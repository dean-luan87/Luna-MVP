closure/assimilation integration.

Reasons:

1. Its `responsibility_domain=BRAIN` and
   `canonical_owner_status=OWNER_UNRESOLVED` correctly represent the absence
   of a concrete Brain runtime owner.
2. Its lifecycle owner already points to `Cognitive Flow Governance`, which is
   supported for lifecycle mechanics.
3. Its Sufficiency/Stop proof ownership remains
   `Cognitive State Formation Governance`.
4. Its Re-observation ownership remains `Field Perception Orchestrator`.
5. Its closure acceptance and assimilation fields must remain unresolved;
   replacing them would overstate the current contracts.
