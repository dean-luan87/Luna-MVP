# Candidate and Mutation Boundaries

All Brain-domain outputs in the current controlled integration remain
references or candidates.

Brain may coordinate or consume:

- Goal, Intent, Concern, Context references;
- admitted Information Need references when a future admission contract
  exists;
- A-Route execution and canonical cognition results;
- Sufficiency, Gap, Re-observation, Revision, and Stop references;
- Closure and Assimilation Candidates.

Brain may not mutate:

- Intent or Context;
- Field or evidence admission;
- Attention, Hypothesis, Current World, Sufficiency, Gap, Revision, or Stop;
- Memory, Experience, Knowledge, Learning, or Self state;
- Decision, Task, Action, model/provider state;
- World Truth.

`CognitiveFlowOutputV1` is candidate-only and source-owner mutation is false.
`BrainAssimilationCandidateV1` is also candidate-only and carries explicit
false mutation/execution guards. Existing downstream registries remain the
only possible mutation authorities for their respective state domains.
