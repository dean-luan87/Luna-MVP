# Luna Capability Survival Principle v1

## Adaptive Survival Principle

Luna governs Capability evolution through three simultaneous commitments:

1. **Core Stability** — external Capabilities cannot contaminate core cognition: Evidence is not Fact, candidates are not Decisions/Actions/States, and Reducer mutation authority remains unique.
2. **Adaptive Evolution** — Capability forms, configurations, combinations, providers, and contextual specializations may evolve when their scope is declared and compatible.
3. **Controlled Mutation** — meaningful change is traceable, assessable, validated, reversible where compatible, and subject to Constitution, Protocol, and Permission/Admission boundaries.

## Survival Does Not Mean Autonomy

A high-fitness Capability is not entitled to activation, preference, replacement of a peer, permanence, or broader authority. A low-fitness Capability is not automatically retired. Survival-oriented governance supplies evidence for human/governed review while preserving diversity, fallback options, and history.

## Capability Health Observation

Diagnostics evolves from error detection to Capability Health Observation. It may **observe**, **evaluate**, and **recommend** on drift, compatibility, risk, resource pressure, failure patterns, fitness signals, adaptation history, and deprecated usage. It may not **decide**, **activate**, **replace**, **retire**, **upgrade**, or **grant permission**.
