# Background Cognitive Process Model v1

## Background candidates

Background processes may maintain:

- Field Stability Monitor;
- Capability Health;
- Memory/Experience Maintenance;
- Unknown Tracking;
- Self Resource Awareness;
- stale-state and provenance diagnostics.

They run at lower frequency candidates than safety or foreground work and may
be suspended under resource pressure. They never silently become Brain or
Foreground Decision processes.

## Escalation boundary

Background output is a Diagnostic Candidate, Stability Candidate, Unknown
Update Candidate, or Brain Escalation Candidate. It cannot choose a Goal,
interpret a value, modify Reality, or execute Action.

Background maintenance cannot erase Unknown, replace current Evidence with
Experience, or automatically learn a new policy. It cannot interpret Reality,
cannot execute Action, and produces an Unknown Update Candidate.
