# Brain Activation Boundary v1

## Activation conditions

Brain Activation Candidate may be raised by:

- unresolved conflict;
- high or persistent Unknown;
- survival/risk escalation;
- Intent drift candidate;
- Goal conflict candidate;
- resource trade-off beyond routine policy;
- repeated failure or capability limitation;
- a Field transition requiring final evaluation.

Routine state refresh, stable low-risk observation, and ordinary background
maintenance do not require Brain activation.

## Contract

```text
Conflict / Unknown High / Risk
          ↓
Brain Activation Candidate
          ↓
Brain Evaluation
```

Activation is a request for evaluation, not a Decision. Brain retains Intent,
Goal, Value, and final Decision authority. Runtime, Neural, Attention, or
Capability cannot activate Brain by changing its Goal. Brain retains Goal authority.
