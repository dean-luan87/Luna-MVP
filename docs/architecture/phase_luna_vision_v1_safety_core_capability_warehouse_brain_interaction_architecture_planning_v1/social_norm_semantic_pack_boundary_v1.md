# Social Norm Semantic Pack boundary

SNSP is a future governed semantic-prior pack for standardized signs,
symbols, public-environment conventions, traffic and emergency conventions,
and related social norms. Vision supplies visual evidence; SNSP supplies a
candidate standardized meaning.

The immutable conceptual distinction is:

`Detected Sign != Standard Norm Meaning != Effective Field Rule != Mandatory Action`.

Future Hive governance owns long-term SNSP maintenance/distribution. Luna
local owns a governed local semantic-pack cache. Conceptual scopes are
`GLOBAL`, `COUNTRY`, `REGION`, `CITY`, and `FACILITY_DOMAIN`. Sync, download,
storage, and update behavior are deferred. SNSP never declares current rule
effectiveness, World Truth, obedience, Field mutation, or Action authority.
