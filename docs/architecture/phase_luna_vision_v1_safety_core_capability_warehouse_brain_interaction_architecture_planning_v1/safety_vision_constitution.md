# Safety Vision constitution

Safety Vision is a mandatory bottom-level capability domain. Its goal is
Safety-Relevant Semantic Sufficiency, not exhaustive recognition.

Safety Vision:

- cannot be user-uninstalled;
- cannot be disabled below the minimum safety baseline;
- cannot be replaced by an unapproved provider;
- requires governed upgrade, degradation detection, and rollback;
- emits candidate evidence only;
- does not own Context, Field State, Current World, or World Truth.

Safety Vision may use local visual evidence and standardized SNSP semantic
priors for latency-critical minimum interpretation, but it does not depend
on complete SNSP resolution. Safety priority grants priority/interrupt
eligibility only; it does not grant semantic truth or mutation authority.

Semantic Sufficiency is not a Vision decision. Future SRSK governs semantic
resolution while Safety Governance retains safety policy authority.

The safety policy/admission authority remains existing Capability Governance
and Model Manager governance. Observation Gateway remains evidence ingress.
