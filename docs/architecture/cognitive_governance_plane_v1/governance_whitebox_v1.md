# Cognitive Governance Plane Whitebox v1

## Lifecycle trace

```text
Constitution Check
        ↓
Change Proposal / Object Draft
        ↓
Protocol + Registry Review
        ↓
Admission
        ↓
Authority / Resource Review
        ↓
Diagnostics Baseline
        ↓
Active Candidate
        ↓
Runtime Governance
        ↓
Deprecated / Archived
```

For every object the trace answers: Who creates? Who approves? Who modifies?
Who runs? Who observes? Who can revoke?

## Boundary assertions

Registry says what exists; Admission says whether it may enter; Authority says
who may act; Diagnostics says what is abnormal; Resource Governance says what
budget is available; Runtime Governance says how mechanics may run. None says
what Luna should want.

Constitution remains highest constraint. Brain remains final cognitive
authority. Reducer remains the sole State mutation authority. Diagnostics emits
Candidate, not Action. No governance component directly mutates Reality, Goal,
Decision, Value, or Self Identity.

Authority says who may act. Brain remains final cognitive authority. Diagnostic
Candidate is not Action. No direct Reality mutation, No direct Goal mutation,
No direct Decision mutation, and no second Brain are permitted.

Diagnostic Candidate is not Action.
