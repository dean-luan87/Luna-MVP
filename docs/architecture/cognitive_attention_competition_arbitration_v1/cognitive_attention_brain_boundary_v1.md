# Cognitive Attention Brain Boundary v1

## Authority split

Attention tells Brain what is currently worth attention. Brain evaluates the
candidate and retains final Goal, Value, and Decision authority:

```text
Attention Candidate
      ↓
Brain Evaluation
      ↓
Decision Candidate
```

Attention cannot decide what Luna should do, set a Goal, issue an Action,
change Reality, change Value, or redefine Self. Brain may accept, modify,
reject, or defer an Attention Candidate.

## Neural boundary

Neural Fast Attention can surface an Attention Interrupt Request from new
Evidence. It can provide fast resource and anomaly candidates but cannot
replace Brain Evaluation or A Route Reality Understanding.

## Future interfaces

Role and Emotion may later supply Attention Bias Candidate. They do not receive
direct Attention control in this phase. Social Field, B Route, and multi-A
coordination are outside scope.

Attention cannot issue an Action and cannot change Reality. Brain Evaluation
remains the final authority.
