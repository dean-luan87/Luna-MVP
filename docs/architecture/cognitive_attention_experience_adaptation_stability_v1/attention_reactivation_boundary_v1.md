# Attention Reactivation Boundary v1

## Reactivation chain

```text
Stable Pattern
      ↓
Reality Deviation
      ↓
Attention Reactivation Candidate
      ↓
Increase Observation Candidate
```

New Evidence, confidence decline, risk increase, environmental change, or
Capability Change may invalidate a stable attention assumption. Reactivation
must retain trigger Evidence, provenance, scope, uncertainty, and affected
Field.

## No blind familiarity

Stable Pattern cannot suppress an anomaly, erase Unknown, or force a reduced
observation. Current Reality and current Self Capability are checked before a
Reduced Observation Candidate is used.

Reactivation is a candidate resource adjustment. It does not directly modify
Attention, Field, Reality, Goal, Decision, or Action, and it does not perform
automatic learning.

Stable Pattern cannot erase Unknown. Reactivation does not directly modify Attention and does not perform automatic learning.
