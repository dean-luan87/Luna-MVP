# Cognitive Attention Experience Adaptation and Stability Model v1

## Scope

This phase governs how Field Experience may shape a future Attention Prior
Candidate without becoming an online learning system or automatically changing
behavior. Experience shapes attention only through a validated candidate.

```text
Field Experience
      ↓
Pattern Extraction
      ↓
Attention Prior Candidate
      ↓
Attention Allocation Adjustment Candidate
      ↓
Future Attention
```

## Core boundary

Experience Candidate enters an Attention Pattern Candidate boundary. Experience
cannot directly modify Attention, Brain, Goal, Decision, Self Identity, or
Reality. A single event cannot create a permanent attention preference. Pattern
Extraction requires Field Context, observed conditions, provenance, outcome
feedback, confidence, scope, and validation status.

## Field-based adaptation

Field Attention Memory stores Field Context, Condition, Observed Pattern,
Attention Requirement, and Outcome Feedback. A new airport entry may produce a
High Attention Candidate; a stable commute may produce a Reduced Observation
Candidate. Both remain scoped candidates and must respect current Reality.

## Stability and correction

Familiarity progresses through Unknown Field, Learning Field, Stable Field, and
Changed Field. Stable does not mean closed. New Evidence and Reality Deviation
produce an Attention Reactivation Candidate. Attention Drift Candidate detects
when historical patterns cause attraction, persistence, narrowing, omission, or
overgeneralization drift.

Pattern Strength includes Experience Frequency, Validation Confidence, and Time
Decay. An old event cannot retain maximum priority forever.

## Capability and future B boundary

Capability Change may produce an Attention Requirement Candidate and an
Observation Strategy Candidate, such as compensating audio or Human Feedback
when visual capability declines. A future Simulation Field may consume a
Historical Attention Pattern, but B Route is a placeholder only.

## Prohibitions

No automatic learning, no automatic attention policy change, no Brain
modification, no Goal modification, no Decision, no Action, no Emotion Runtime,
no Role Runtime, no Social Runtime, no B Runtime, and no Model Training are
implemented.

Time Decay is explicit. No automatic attention policy change, No Decision, No
Action, and No Model Training are enabled.

No Action is enabled in this phase.
