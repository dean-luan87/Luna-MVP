# Attention Familiarity Model v1

## Familiarity states

```text
Unknown Field
      ↓
Learning Field
      ↓
Stable Field
      ↓
Changed Field
```

Unknown Field has high Unknown and high initial Attention demand. Learning Field
uses validated observations to form scoped candidates. Stable Field may reduce
resource investment, but Stable does not mean closed. Changed Field reopens
uncertainty and increases attention.

## Situated familiarity

Familiarity depends on Field Context, time, location, Self Capability, current
Goal, Reality Stability, and evidence quality. It does not transfer blindly to
another Field or another Self state.

Repeated airport entry may lower normal observation cost. A new closure, sound,
construction event, or capability decline produces a Changed Field candidate.
Current Evidence has priority over familiarity and historical experience.

## Boundary

Familiarity produces Attention Prior Candidate and Reduced Observation
Candidate only. It cannot directly close observation, alter Reality, make a
Decision, issue an Action, or modify an Attention Policy.

A Familiarity candidate cannot make a Decision.

Reduced Observation Candidate is a candidate only.
