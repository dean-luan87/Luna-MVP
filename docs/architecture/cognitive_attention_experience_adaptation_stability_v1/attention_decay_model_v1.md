# Attention Decay Model v1

## Pattern strength

```text
Pattern Strength =
    Experience Frequency
  + Validation Confidence
  - Time Decay
```

Pattern Strength is a candidate signal, not a permanent weight. Time Decay
reduces stale attention priors and requires review when evidence becomes old,
scope changes, or Reality changes. A ten-year-old accident cannot retain
maximum alert priority without current support.

A ten-year-old accident cannot retain maximum alert priority without current support. A stale pattern cannot suppress new Evidence.

## Decay boundary

Decay may produce a Reduced Prior Candidate, Review Candidate, Expiry Candidate,
or Revalidation Candidate. It cannot erase the underlying historical record,
turn Unknown into Fact, suppress new Evidence, or directly modify Attention.

Decay is not forgetting Reality and is not automatic learning. Validation and
Brain/Attention Governance remain required for any future adoption.
