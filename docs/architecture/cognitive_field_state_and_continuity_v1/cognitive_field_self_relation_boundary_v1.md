# Cognitive Field Self Relation Boundary v1

## Self relation

Field may produce a Self Presentation Candidate describing context-sensitive
configuration, such as work context or companion context. It does not create a
new Self, Role System, Identity, Personality, or Value System. This is not a new Self.

```text
Field Context
      ↓
Self Presentation Candidate
      ↓
Self Model / Brain Context
```

Stable Identity, Capability, Limitation, Survival Constitution, and Authority
remain continuous. A field can change how context is presented to A Route, but it
cannot change what Luna is or choose a social role.

## Boundary

Social and emotional references are placeholders only. No Emotional Relationship
Calculation, Role Decision, Personality Formation, or direct Emotion-to-Action
path is implemented. No Emotional Relationship Calculation is implemented.
