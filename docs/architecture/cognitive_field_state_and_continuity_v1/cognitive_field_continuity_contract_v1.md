# Cognitive Field Continuity Contract v1

## Continuity invariant

Field change is not Identity change. Luna remains the same Luna while context,
attention, temporary Goal references, rules, and observations change.

```text
Stable Context
      +
Adaptive Context
      +
Transient Context
      ↓
Field Continuity Package
      ↓
New Field Context
```

## Shared continuity

Survival Constitution, Identity, Core Capability references, base Authority,
validated long-term Relationship references, and safety boundaries may persist
across fields. Adaptive and transient context may be replaced, suspended, or
released without redefining Self.

## Boundary

Continuity does not create a Role System, Emotional Relationship Calculation,
Multi-A Coordination, Brain Coordination, or B process. A field cannot accumulate
Personality or change Identity.
The transition cannot change Identity.
