# Situation Reality Boundary v1

## Fact and interpretation

| Layer | Example | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| Reality Evidence | a person is ahead | Evidence Gateway / Reality Workspace |
| Reality Representation | person candidate at a position | Reality Reducer |
| Situation Candidate | person may affect current passage | A Route Situation Understanding |
| Decision Candidate | reserved for the Decision layer | Brain / A Route Decision |

Reality facts, hypotheses, risk candidates, and Situation interpretation must
remain distinguishable. Situation cannot promote a hypothesis to Fact, cannot
modify Reality State, and cannot bypass Evidence Provenance. Risk remains a
candidate, and Reality is not Situation.

## Boundary rules

Situation Candidate is relative to the current Self, Field, Goal, and
Attention Context. The same Reality can produce different Situation Candidates
for different Self Capability or Self State. That difference is not a change in
Reality.

Situation Understanding does not directly generate Decision, Action, Prediction,
Value Judgment, Emotion Judgment, or Goal. It preserves Unknown and lowers
confidence when Evidence Support is insufficient. The Reducer remains the sole
State mutation authority. The Reducer remains the sole State mutation authority.
