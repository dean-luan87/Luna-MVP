# Situation Experience Boundary v1

## Experience relationship

Experience Reference may assist Situation Understanding with a contextual
prior, familiarity, or known pattern. Current Field and Current Goal are
required context bindings:

```text
Experience Reference
        ↓
Situation Prior Candidate
        + Current Reality Evidence
        + Current Self / Field / Goal
        ↓
Situation Candidate
```

Experience cannot override Current Reality Evidence, cannot turn a hypothesis
into Fact, and cannot directly modify Situation State, Reality, Goal, Decision,
or Action. `Reality > Experience` remains the governing precedence.

One familiar route cannot make a changed route safe by default. A new Evidence
deviation must trigger Situation Reassessment Candidate. Experience is a
reference, not automatic learning, online learning, or a replacement for
Unknown.
