# Negative Guards

The controlled evaluation enforces:

- no new admission owner;
- no Gateway submission or runtime admission execution;
- no FPO runtime invocation;
- no Provider or Model binding, selection, or invocation;
- no capability activation or slot reservation;
- no resource scheduling;
- no camera, OCR, SLAM, VLM, or observation execution;
- no evidence ingress or fusion;
- no Attention, Decision, Task, or Action;
- no ranking, scoring, priority, winner, fallback, or semantic inference;
- no Current World, Field, Memory, or PCN mutation;
- no direct Routing Candidate → Gateway bypass;
- no provider/model fabrication when the Gateway contract cannot yet be met.

Capability admission and FPO compatibility do not imply Runtime Admission.
