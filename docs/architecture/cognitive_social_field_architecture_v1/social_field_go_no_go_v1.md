# Social Field Go / No-Go v1

## Required readiness checks

- Social Field is a contextual social operating environment, not a contact
  list, social network, face recognition system, or relationship database.
- Participants include Identity Candidate, Observation Evidence, Confidence,
  Field Presence, and Interaction History.
- Role belongs to Field, not Identity, and includes permission, responsibility,
  expected behavior, and confidence.
- Relationship is bound to participants, context, time, history, confidence,
  and boundary.
- Social Rules include Norm, Authority, Interaction, Cultural, and Temporary
  candidates with Evidence, Confidence, and Unknown.
- Interaction Pattern is historical observation, not Prediction.
- Social Field keeps Identity Continuity and does not create Decision.
- Memory does not override Reality and Emotion is placeholder-only.

## Explicit prohibitions

No Emotion Runtime, No Personality Switching, No Social Decision, No
Relationship Judgment, No Human Identity Confirmation, No Face Recognition
Runtime, No Multi-Agent, No Hive, No B Route, No Prediction, No Action Runtime,
and No Social Runtime. Social Field cannot directly modify Reality, Identity,
Goal, Decision, or Action.

The Agent performs V0 static checks only, does not run the Final Phase Verifier,
and stops at `WAITING_FOR_USER_TERMINAL_VERIFICATION`.

This is not a contact list. No Relationship Judgment. No Face Recognition
Runtime.
