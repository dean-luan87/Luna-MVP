# Social Field Whitebox v1

```text
Physical Field + Social Evidence
             ↓
       Social Field
             ↓
Participants / Role / Relationship / Social Rule / Interaction Pattern
             ↓
Attention Candidate / Behavior Candidate / Memory Candidate
```

## Control questions

- Who creates a Participant? Observation Evidence creates an Identity
  Candidate; it never confirms Human Identity.
- Who assigns a Role? Field Context and governed Role Candidate; Role belongs
  to Field, not Identity.
- Who forms Relationship? Contextual Participant evidence and Interaction
  History; it is not an Emotion or Judgment engine.
- Who validates Social Rule? Evidence, Confidence, Unknown, and governance;
  Social Rule is a candidate, not absolute Reality.
- Who owns Goal and Decision? Brain; Social Field has no Social Decision
  authority.
- Who modifies Reality? Reality Reducer after Evidence Gateway; Social Field
  has no Reality Write authority.

## Isolation invariants

Social Field → Attention Candidate, Social Field → Behavior Candidate, and
Social Field → Social Field Memory Candidate are permitted. Social Field →
Emotion is placeholder-only. Social Field → Decision, Relationship → Emotion
Judgment, Role → Identity Mutation, Participant → Human Identity Confirmation,
and Interaction Pattern → Prediction are forbidden.

Unknown, Confidence, Evidence, provenance, Field Context, and time remain
attached to all social candidates. This is a Planning Only artifact.

Identity Candidate is provisional. Role belongs to Field. Interaction History
is contextual. Social Field → Emotion is placeholder-only. Relationship →
Emotion Judgment is forbidden.

Relationship → Emotion Judgment is forbidden.
