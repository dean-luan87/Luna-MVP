# Experience Transition Model v1

```text
Outcome
   ↓
Outcome Evaluation Candidate
   ↓
Experience Candidate
   ↓
Reinforced / Rejected / Deferred Candidate
   ↓
Memory Candidate or Schema Candidate
```

Outcome is a fact with evidence. Experience is a situated interpretation that
captures what happened, what mattered, and what may be useful later. A single
event may remain an episode and never become long-term Memory. Repetition,
impact, confidence, and conflict status are required before folding into a
pattern or Schema candidate.

The Information Lifecycle remains the governing retention and folding boundary.
No automatic learning, consolidation, pattern mining, or Self modification is
implemented in this phase.
