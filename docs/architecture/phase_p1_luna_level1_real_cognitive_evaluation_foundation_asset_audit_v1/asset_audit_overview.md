## Executive finding

Luna has a usable evaluation-only foundation, but not a complete first real Level-1 cognitive evaluation path.

- Dataset Registry: canonical evaluation-owned declaration and explicit registration workflow exist; no real dataset or sample is registered.
- Level-1 suite: canonical declarative categories, layered GT refs, assertions, perturbations, and sample-to-case composition exist; persistent real case registration and registry-membership enforcement are not established.
- A-Route cognition: the available Cognitive State Formation, A-Route, and active-observation integrations are synthetic/candidate-only controlled implementations. The Roboflow smoke proves one narrow provider-to-candidate chain, not a general real Luna A-Route.
- White-box: `CognitiveWhiteBoxTraceV1`, `LunaCognitiveExecutionProfileV1`, and `CognitiveFailureGapRefV1` exist and are candidate-only. They capture synthetic traces and are not yet connected to a general runtime collector.
- Archive/comparison: `_eval_out`, `_tmp_eval_out`, TestBoard artifacts, and report envelopes exist as evidence surfaces. No canonical durable evaluation archive or same-case Luna-version baseline comparison store was found.

The principal P0 blocker is the absence of an evidenced, real, evidence-driven A-Route cognitive runtime reachable from a registered Level-1 case. Durable archive, real registration, and comparison are P1 gaps.

## Audit method

The audit classified assets by semantic ownership and observed implementation behavior, not by filename. Historical planning documents were treated as claims or precedent. Generated artifacts were treated as evidence only.
