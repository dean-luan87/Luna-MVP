# Field / Role Interaction Risk Review

This review records planning risks only. It does not authorize implementation or modify a frozen Owner.

1. **Field treated as a physical place.** Guard: Field projections may include physical, social, task, temporal, relationship, and subjective dimensions; a place label is never the complete Field.
2. **Field Boundary reduced to a binary inside/outside flag.** Guard: FUZZY boundaries retain Core, Strong, Fuzzy Edge, and Weak Association regions; HARD boundaries remain explicit external constraints.
3. **Temporary Occupation mutates a Core Field.** Guard: temporary or repeated occupation produces projection and influence candidates only; structural change requires source-owner governance.
4. **Multiple Roles forced into one winner.** Guard: simultaneous Role projections are valid; competition is a candidate and later Decision Arbitration, subject to Self boundaries, owns selection.
5. **A new Relationship overwrites an old Relationship.** Guard: every relationship instance has an independent identifier and lifecycle; the same pair may hold multiple relationships.
6. **Historical Relationship deleted.** Guard: an ended relationship may become Historical, Dormant, or Reactivatable; end is not delete.
7. **Resonance promoted to Causal Fact.** Guard: resonance outputs only similarity, historical projection, and activation candidates; Causal Governance remains future and separate.
8. **Interaction Kernel absorbed by PCN.** Guard: PCN owns links, activation, and projection; the kernel owns only ephemeral Local Interaction Candidates.
9. **Interaction Kernel implemented as a CNN.** Guard: “convolution-like” is a conceptual local-interaction metaphor only; CNN, GNN, neural network, and mathematical implementation are out of scope.
10. **Field Competition simplified to score-versus-score winner selection.** Guard: competition preserves Field, Role, Goal, Relationship, Benefit/Cost, uncertainty, and hard constraints; no arbitration occurs here.
11. **Resource governance replaced by fixed numeric limits.** Guard: no universal maximum is frozen for Fields, Roles, overlaps, history, or interaction depth; resource pressure shrinks scope rather than deleting core objects.
12. **Nested-loop living-system structure lost.** Guard: every cross-object relation records incoming constraint, outgoing constraint, feedback, mutation Owner, and projection authority; source owners remain authoritative.

Residual risk remains at future skeleton design: a developer could mistake candidate projection for state mutation. The future skeleton must preserve candidate-only types, source references, explicit provenance, and no-second-writer checks.
