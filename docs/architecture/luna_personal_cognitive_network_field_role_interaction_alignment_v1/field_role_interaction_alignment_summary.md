# PCN Field / Role Interaction Alignment Summary

## Outcome

This planning phase aligns the existing PCN candidate architecture with Field, Role, Relationship, Context, Resource, and Observation Axis boundaries without changing any existing asset. It introduces no Runtime, persistence, algorithm, neural model, skeleton, migration, or active contract.

## Alignment Model

- **Field Boundary:** HARD boundaries come only from explicit external constraints and remain source-owned. FUZZY boundaries describe graded cognitive participation from Core Region through Weak Association.
- **Role Boundary:** Role Core, Strong Projection, Fuzzy Projection, and Candidate Projection coexist under Field, Context, Relationship, Self, and resource constraints.
- **Occupation:** Temporary Dominant Projection is distinct from Core Field Ownership. Repetition may yield Persistent Influence Candidate, never automatic structural mutation.
- **Expansion / Contraction:** interaction scope expands or contracts under Resource Governance. No fixed global cardinality is frozen, and contraction never deletes a core Field, Role, or Relationship.
- **Resonance:** structural similarity across role, relationship, task, benefit/cost, experience, and context may activate related projections; resonance is not causal proof.
- **Competition:** simultaneous projections may create a Competition Candidate, but this layer does not arbitrate a winner.
- **Overlap / Coexistence:** multiple Fields, Roles, and Relationships may overlap or coexist without default conflict or fusion.
- **Historical Reactivation:** dormant or historical Field, Role, and Relationship references may reactivate as projections while current Reality retains precedence.
- **Interaction Kernel:** a conceptual local-interaction lens emits Local Interaction Candidates. It is separate from PCN ownership and is not a CNN, GNN, causal engine, or decision engine.
- **Observation Axis:** selects the perspective; the Interaction Kernel observes local responses within that perspective. Neither replaces the other.
- **PCN Impact:** existing Object, Link, Activation, Dormancy, Growth, Nested Constraint, and Scenario assets are compatible or require only later authorized reference/skeleton accounting. None is modified now.
- **Resource Constraint:** bounded scope is policy-driven, not a fixed object-count limit.
- **Nested Constraint:** every relation declares incoming and outgoing constraints, feedback, mutation Owner, and projection authority, including inactive future Emotion, Causal, and Decision placeholders.
- **Stress Test:** eight candidate scenarios cover occupation, persistent influence, multiple relationships, ended-but-retained relationships, historical reactivation, resonance, role competition, overlap, and coexistence.

## Canonical Ownership

PCN owns cross-object links, activation candidates, and context projections. Cognitive Interaction Governance owns only ephemeral Local Interaction Candidates. Field, Role, Relationship, Context, Self, Memory, Value, Resource, Observation Axis, and future Causal/Decision owners retain their existing authority. There is no second writer.

## Stop Boundary

The next phase is not authorized. Interaction mathematics, graph algorithms, CNN/GNN/neural implementation, persistence, Runtime, PCN skeleton, and migration remain prohibited. Final verification belongs to the user terminal; V3 audit belongs to ChatGPT.
