# Attention Familiarity Model v1

## Familiarity relation

```text
Unknown high + new Field
        ↓
Attention high investment candidate
        ↓
Stable Reality Pattern + confidence
        ↓
Attention cost reduction candidate
```

Familiarity is situated and scoped to a Field, time, Self Capability, and
evidence quality. Familiarity does not mean Reality is permanently safe or
known. Confidence may lower attention demand only when the pattern remains
validated.

## Deviation rule

```text
Stable Pattern
      ↓
New Evidence
      ↓
Deviation Detection
      ↓
Attention Increase Candidate
```

Construction, unexpected motion, capability degradation, or a confidence drop
can re-activate attention. Current Evidence has priority over stale familiarity
and Experience.

## Boundary

The Familiarity Model emits candidates only. It cannot suppress Unknown,
modify Reality, make a Decision, issue an Action, or automatically change an
Attention Policy.

The Familiarity Model cannot make a Decision, cannot issue an Action, and
cannot automatically change an Attention Policy.
