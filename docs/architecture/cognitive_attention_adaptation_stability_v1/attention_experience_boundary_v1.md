# Attention Experience Boundary v1

## Experience handoff

```text
Outcome
    ↓
Experience Candidate
    ↓
Attention Pattern Candidate
    ↓
Validation
    ↓
Future Allocation Candidate
```

Experience is a reference, not direct Attention control. The handoff must
retain Situation, Self Capability, Reality context, Decision/Outcome trace,
cause, confidence, uncertainty, and scope. Current Reality overrides stale
Experience.

## Adoption boundary

Validation may reject, defer, expire, narrow, or adopt an Attention Pattern
Candidate. Adoption is not automatic learning and does not directly modify an
Attention Policy. No single event may change a long-term strategy.

Experience cannot modify Brain, Goal, Self Identity, Reality State, Decision,
or Action. B Route and Reflection are future consumers only.

Current Reality overrides stale Experience. Experience cannot modify Goal and
cannot modify Self Identity.
