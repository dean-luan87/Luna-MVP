# Cognitive Attention Adaptation and Stability Model v1

## Scope

This phase defines how long-term experience may shape an Attention Pattern
Candidate without becoming automatic learning or an automatic policy change.
It governs stability, familiarity, anomaly reactivation, drift detection, and
candidate validation. It does not implement Runtime.

```text
Experience
    ↓
Attention Pattern Candidate
    ↓
Validation
    ↓
Future Allocation Candidate
```

One event cannot change an attention strategy, baseline, Goal, Brain, or Self
Model. A pattern requires provenance, repeated support or explicit validation,
confidence, scope, expiry/review condition, and known exceptions.

## Familiarity and stability

An unfamiliar Field has high Unknown and high Attention demand. A familiar,
stable Field can lower Attention Allocation and release resources. Familiarity
never suppresses new Evidence: deviation detection may produce an Attention
Increase Candidate and re-activate attention.

## Candidate-only adaptation

Adaptation may propose a lower or higher observation cost, a changed attention
axis, a compensatory observation, or a drift correction candidate. Validation
must compare Reality, current Field, Self Capability, and Experience. Experience
does not directly control Attention and cannot replace current Reality.

## Current boundary

No online learning, no automatic strategy modification, no automatic policy
update, no Emotion Runtime, no Role Runtime, no B Route, no Decision, no Action,
and no Runtime are implemented. Brain and existing Attention Governance retain
authority.

Unknown high leads to Attention high investment. Stable patterns permit an
Attention cost reduction candidate after Deviation Detection and an Attention
Increase Candidate. Experience does not directly control Attention. No
automatic strategy modification, No Emotion Runtime, No Role Runtime, No B
Route, No Decision, No Action, and No Runtime are enabled.

Attention Increase Candidate is a candidate only. No automatic strategy modification is enabled. No B Route is enabled.
