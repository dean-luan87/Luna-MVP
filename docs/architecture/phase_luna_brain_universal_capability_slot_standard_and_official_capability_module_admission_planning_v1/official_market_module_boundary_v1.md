# Official and Market Module boundary

Current scope is `origin = OFFICIAL`, sourced through Luna Official /
Hive-governed authority. Future Module origin may include `MARKET`, but Market
Capability Module Governance is deferred.

Future Market Modules must reuse the Universal Capability Slot standard unless
a future constitutional decision changes it. This phase does not define a
marketplace, SDK, publishing, billing, commercial review, third-party trust,
third-party admission, or sandbox system.

`MARKET_CAPABILITY_MODULE_GOVERNANCE_DEFERRED` is explicit.
