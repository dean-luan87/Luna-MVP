# Survival-first capability regulation

Future regulation must preserve a Minimum Survival Capability Configuration:
the minimum sufficient capability configuration required for continued safe
existence and current critical objectives.

Under pressure, ordinary capabilities may be suspended, unloaded, or have an
implementation released when governance permits. Mandatory Safety Modules
cannot be removed because of low usage, low historical contribution, or a
resource optimization candidate. Safety constitutional value outranks
ordinary capability value.

Optimization algorithms and resource scoring are deferred.
