# Cross-Constraint Precedence Resolution v1

## Authority

**Brain global governance** owns precedence when Safety, Permission, Grant, Resource and local priority conflict. Safety, Permission and Resource Governance retain their domain policy and version authority. This is a global ordering contract, not a new Constraint Manager.

## Conceptual precedence

1. Hard Safety prohibition or emergency override.
2. Hard Permission prohibition or revocation.
3. Grant scope and source-version validity.
4. Protected Resource ceiling or reserve.
5. Resource degradation and optimization.
6. Local Attention, Task and Decision priority.

## Enforcement

Working Envelope, Decision, Task, Runtime Admission, Provider, Observation and Action enforce supplied refs at their own boundaries. They do not rewrite precedence or domain policy. A and Outcome interpret semantic consequences; Brain interprets global consequences.

## Conflict and responsibility

Conflicting refs produce an explicit blocked, degraded or uncertain governance result with provenance. Brain is responsible for incorrect global precedence. Safety/Permission/Resource owners are responsible for incorrect domain policy. The boundary that fails to enforce a valid current ref owns enforcement failure. Attention, Task and Decision cannot override a higher constraint.
