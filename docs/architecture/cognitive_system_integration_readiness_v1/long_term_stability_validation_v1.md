# Long-Term Stability Validation v1

## Validation horizon

The integration blueprint is evaluated across 24 hours, 7 days, and 30 days
using synthetic state descriptions only. This is not Runtime Execution.

## Scenarios

- Day 1: an unfamiliar city creates a Field with high Unknown and elevated
  Attention Observation candidates.
- Day 7: a familiar route may lower repeated Observation Cost while preserving
  anomaly reactivation and Safety Attention.
- Day 30: an environmental change creates a Field and Attention reactivation
  candidate; Experience does not overwrite Reality.

## Stability invariants

Identity, Constitution, and core Capability remain continuous across Field
transitions. Task continuity can be Active, Background, Suspended, Resumed,
Completed, Abandoned, or Archived. Unknown remains explicit. Attention Pattern
Candidates decay and require validation. State growth is bounded by lifecycle,
compression, provenance, and archival boundaries.

No long-running implementation, Scheduler, model call, hardware call, action,
Emotion Runtime, Social Runtime, or B Runtime is executed by this validation.

The horizon preserves Self State and Experience Candidates. No Scheduler, No
model call, No hardware call, and No action are executed.
