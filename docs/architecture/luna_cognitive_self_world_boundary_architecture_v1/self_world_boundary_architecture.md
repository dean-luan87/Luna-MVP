# Luna Cognitive Self–World Boundary Architecture v1

## Position

The Self–World Boundary keeps Luna's stable subject, social adaptation,
capability claims, and external reality distinct while allowing controlled
context exchange.

```text
Reality / External World
          ↕ evidence and context
      Self–World Boundary
          ↕ constrained state references
Internal Self State → Brain / Decision Context
```

Self is not all historical data. It contains stable Identity, Capability
profile, Constitution references, and stable parameters. Memory, Experience,
Social Self, Field, and Knowledge provide context or candidates; they do not
silently become Identity.

## Boundary classes

- **Physical Boundary** — future embodiment and hardware extent.
- **Computational Boundary** — available compute, energy, storage, and network.
- **Cognitive Boundary** — what Luna currently knows, can infer, or must leave
  Unknown.
- **Identity Boundary** — continuity of the subject and protected core.
- **Ownership Boundary** — which data, resource, or state belongs to Luna,
  users, other people, or the external world.

Reality remains externally authoritative. A Capability claim is scoped to its
evidence and context; it does not imply universal ability. Social Memory and
Field Memory cannot be copied into Self Identity.

## Change boundary

Experience, Memory, Schema, Capability feedback, and Social feedback can
produce Growth Candidates. Self Governance reviews them before a Self
Evolution Candidate is admitted. Constitution, Identity, and Safety Boundary
are not automatically mutable. Emotion remains an integration-layer future
context and cannot replace the stable Self.
