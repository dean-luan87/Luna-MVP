# PCN Integrated Scenario Validation and Planning Closure Summary

Status: `PLANNING_CANDIDATE`

## Closure outcome

The existing Personal Cognitive Network Planning and Field/Role Interaction Alignment are jointly coherent. Their combination supports Context-driven local activation, multiple Field/Role/Relationship projections, temporary occupation, resonance, overlap, competition candidates, historical reactivation, dormancy, subjective personal cognition, resource-bounded degradation, a separate Interaction Kernel, and nested feedback without changing source ownership.

The derived planning decision is `READY_FOR_CONTROLLED_SKELETON`. This means the architecture is sufficient for a separately authorized Controlled Skeleton phase; it is not implementation authorization and does not start that phase.

## Integrated architecture findings

- **Multi-Field / Multi-Role / Multi-Relationship:** concurrent projections remain distinct, source-owned, and eligible for contextual coexistence without forced merge or overwrite.
- **Object / Link Model:** reference-only, many-to-many, subjective, provenance-bearing, and source-owner preserving.
- **Field / Role Boundary:** HARD Field constraints remain external and immutable by projection; fuzzy Field and Role projections remain graded and contextual.
- **Temporary Occupation:** distinct from Core Field ownership and releasable after the driving task/context ends.
- **Resonance:** structural similarity candidate only; never Causal Fact or outcome guarantee.
- **Competition / Overlap:** competition is not arbitration; overlap/coexistence does not imply conflict or automatic fusion.
- **Historical Reactivation / Dormancy:** historical and dormant references remain retained and reactivatable under current context and resources.
- **Subjective Cognition:** biased or wrong personal associations remain representable without truth promotion.
- **Resource Degradation:** scope changes, not source truth, ownership, or persistence.
- **Interaction Kernel:** consumes eight governed inputs and emits only an Interaction Candidate; it owns no PCN, Field, Role, Causal, Decision, or Observation Axis state.
- **Nested Constraints:** twelve current/future relations preserve feedback, constraint, projection, and no-source-mutation rules.
