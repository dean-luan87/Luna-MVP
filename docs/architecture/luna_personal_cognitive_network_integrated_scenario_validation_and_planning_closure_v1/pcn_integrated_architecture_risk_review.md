# PCN Integrated Architecture Risk Review

Status: `PLANNING_CANDIDATE`

This review evaluates the combined PCN Planning and Field/Role Interaction Alignment. It authorizes no implementation.

1. **PCN still resembles an objective Knowledge Graph.** Mitigation: PCN stores source references and subjective relatedness candidates; it is not a truth graph or source-object store.
2. **The model depends on rigid classifications.** Mitigation: states are planning expressions, multiple candidates may coexist, and no fixed FSM or time threshold is active.
3. **Field becomes a place label.** Mitigation: Field includes physical, social, task, temporal, relationship, and subjective projections with current-Reality provenance.
4. **Role becomes a single-choice state.** Mitigation: primary, secondary, weak, dormant, and reactivated projections may coexist; competition does not select a winner.
5. **Multiple relationships overwrite each other.** Mitigation: relationship instances have distinct identifiers and lifecycles; colleague and spouse/former-spouse relations remain separate.
6. **Interaction is absorbed into PCN ownership.** Mitigation: PCN owns links, activation, and projection; Cognitive Interaction Governance owns only Local Interaction Candidates.
7. **Resonance becomes a Causal Fact.** Mitigation: resonance is structural-similarity evidence only and cannot assert cause, outcome, emotion, or truth.
8. **Resource constraints are decorative.** Mitigation: HIGH_RESOURCE and LOW_RESOURCE produce different projection scopes and explicit traces; LOW_RESOURCE cannot delete or mutate source state.
9. **Dormancy equals deletion.** Mitigation: dormant links and historical relationships remain retained, locally reactivatable, and source-owned.
10. **Personal Subjective Network is corrected by objective system rules.** Mitigation: a strong personal link may remain even when wrong; Strength is not Truth and is not Causal Fact.
11. **Nested living-system loops collapse into a pipeline.** Mitigation: Context, Field, Role, Relationship, Memory, Resource, Observation Axis, PCN, and Interaction exchange constraints, feedback, activation, and projections without mutual write.
12. **An architecture cycle creates a second Writer.** Mitigation: every feedback edge preserves source Owner authority; the integrated layer writes candidate metadata only.
13. **Unexplainable structural expansion.** Mitigation: expansion requires contextual relevance, resource admission, temporal validity, provenance, uncertainty, and trace; the minimum sufficient projection is preferred.

Residual implementation risks—graph representation, activation algorithm, strength parameterization, resource-budget translation, and kernel mathematics—are registered as `DEFERRED_IMPLEMENTATION_DETAIL`. Any future proposal that changes Owner, Object Model, Core Boundary, or Source Mutation authority becomes a blocker and requires a separate phase.
