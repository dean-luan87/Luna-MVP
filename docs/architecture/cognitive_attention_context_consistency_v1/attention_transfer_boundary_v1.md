# Attention Transfer Boundary v1

## Transfer candidate

Attention Transfer is permitted only as a candidate when source and target
contexts share relevant structure:

- Field relation or shared environment;
- compatible Condition;
- shared risk or Information Value;
- compatible Self Capability;
- current Reality confirmation;
- provenance and confidence.

Driving safety attention may transfer to road-crossing safety observation.
Work-communication caution cannot directly transfer to family relationship
attention. Similarity does not mean identity.

## Transfer chain

```text
Source Attention Pattern
      ↓
Context Similarity Candidate
      ↓
Transfer Candidate
      ↓
Validation
      ↓
Target Attention Prior Candidate
```

Transfer cannot directly change target Attention, Goal, Brain, Self Identity,
Reality, Decision, or Action. Rejection and Unknown remain traceable.
