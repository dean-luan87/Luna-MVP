# Temporal Continuity Model v1

## Purpose

Temporal Continuity preserves the identity of Goals and Tasks while Reality,
Fields, Self State, Attention, and resources change. It is not a scheduler,
planner, memory dump, or Action Runtime.

## Continuity states

- **Active**: currently eligible for governed cognitive processing.
- **Suspended**: intentionally paused with identity and constraints retained.
- **Resumed**: restored after suspension with a fresh Reality and Field check.
- **Completed**: completion condition supported by outcome evidence.
- **Abandoned**: explicitly reviewed decision to stop pursuing the process.
- **Expired**: validity interval ended without implying Decision Failure.

An interrupted process does not lose its Goal, Task identity, provenance,
pending information, or unknowns. Resumption must revalidate Reality; past
Experience cannot replace current Evidence.

## Cross-day and cross-field continuity

The system may preserve an unfinished hospital task overnight and resume it the
next day. A transition from an office Field to a home Field may place an office
task in Background while retaining its constraints. A new Field never silently
inherits transient confidential context.

## Temporal memory boundary

Persist only continuity-relevant candidates:

- unfinished Goals and Tasks;
- important constraints and validity intervals;
- interruption and resume context;
- completion/outcome evidence references;
- validated Experience candidates.

Transient sensory detail, unresolved speculation, and stale context are not
automatically promoted to durable memory. Unknown remains Unknown until new
Evidence supports an update.

## Prohibitions

Temporal Continuity does not perform automatic planning, automatic execution,
Goal mutation, Decision mutation, Action execution, Emotion, Role, Social
Runtime, B Route, Simulation, or online learning.
