# Task / Experience Boundary v1

Task outcomes may produce an Experience Candidate containing task context,
Decision reference, Action Boundary reference, Outcome Evidence, Cause
Attribution, and adaptation candidate. Experience is reference material, not a
replacement for current Reality.

One failed task does not rewrite Goal, Self Identity, Capability, Decision
Policy, or Task Policy. Repeated outcomes may form a Pattern Candidate only
after validation, with provenance, confidence, and time decay. A successful
outcome does not prove that a risky strategy was correct.

Experience may inform future Attention, Option, or Capability awareness
candidates; it cannot directly change them. Unknowns remain explicit and may
be carried into the next temporal segment.

The Experience boundary does not implement automatic learning, model training,
Emotion, Role, Social Runtime, B Route, Simulation, or automatic planning.

Cause Attribution remains a candidate. Decision Policy is not rewritten by one
task. A successful outcome does not prove a valid strategy.
