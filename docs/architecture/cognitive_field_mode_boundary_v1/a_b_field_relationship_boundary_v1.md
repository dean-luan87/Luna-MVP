# A/B Field Relationship Boundary v1

## Shared base

```text
Reality Field
      ├──────────────→ A Route: Reality understanding
      └──────────────→ B Route placeholder: hypothetical exploration
```

A Route operates in Reality Mode and returns current Situation and Decision
Candidates. B Route, when later authorized, may derive a Simulation Field
Candidate, Historical Reconstruction Field Candidate, or Creative Field Candidate
from a declared base Field.

## One-way responsibility

A supplies reality-grounded Field material. B may produce Strategy Candidate,
Self Understanding Candidate, or Simulation Result Candidate for Brain review. B
does not control current A Decision, does not rewrite facts, and does not share
current Decision authority.

## Current status

B Route is not implemented, Simulation Mode is not active, and only Reality Mode
is allowed. This document freezes the boundary without implementing B.

The Simulation Field Candidate is never a current Reality Field fact.
