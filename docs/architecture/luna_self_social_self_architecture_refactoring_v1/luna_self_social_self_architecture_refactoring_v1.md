# Luna Self / Social Self Architecture Refactoring v1

## New subject model

```text
Luna
├── Self Layer
│   ├── Identity
│   ├── Capability
│   ├── Boundary
│   ├── Resource
│   ├── Regulation
│   └── Evolution
├── Social Self Layer
│   ├── Social Identity
│   ├── Role
│   ├── Relationship
│   ├── Social Norm
│   ├── Responsibility
│   └── Social Adaptation
└── Cognitive Integration Layer
    ├── Internal/External Cognition Integration
    ├── Feedback Classification
    └── Emotion Context Boundary
```

## Self Layer

Self Layer answers: “What am I, am I stable, and can I continue operating?”
It owns identity continuity, capability awareness, internal boundaries,
resources, self regulation, and self evolution. It does not own social roles,
relationships, external evaluation, or social norms.

## Social Self Layer

Social Self answers: “How do I participate in the world?” It owns contextual
social identity candidates, roles, relationships, norms, responsibilities, and
social adaptation. A role is a Field-bound responsibility, not Core Identity or
personality. Social Self cannot rewrite Core Identity, Constitution, or Self
Boundary.

## Integration Layer

Integration decides whether external social feedback is transient context or
evidence relevant to durable Self Evolution. It routes candidates; it does not
directly mutate Self, decide social outcomes, override Brain, or execute
Action.

## Emotion reclassification

Emotion is mounted at the Self-Social Interaction / Integration boundary. The
current artifact is a context placeholder only. It may later provide context
for Attention, Preference, or Relationship; it is not a Decision, Action, Value,
Identity, or Constitution authority.

## Refactoring boundary

This phase changes conceptual ownership mappings only. It does not move,
delete, rename, migrate Memory, refactor code, or implement Runtime, Emotion,
Role, or Social behavior.
