# Minimum observation conditions

`MinimumObservationConditionsV1` belongs to an Observation Requirement, not to
a provider-wide “good view” setting. The controlled v1 vocabulary covers:

`target visibility`, `target completeness`, `relative scale`, `view
orientation`, `stability`, `occlusion`, and `sensor availability`.

The values are categorical candidates such as `VISIBLE`, `COMPLETE`,
`ADEQUATE`, `SUITABLE`, `STABLE`, `CLEAR`, and `AVAILABLE`. No distance,
angle, speed, focal-length, calibration, or other numeric threshold is added.
Unsupported dimensions must remain unavailable rather than being fabricated.
