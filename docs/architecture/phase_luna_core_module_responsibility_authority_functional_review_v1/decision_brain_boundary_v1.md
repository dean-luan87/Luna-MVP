# Brain → Decision Boundary v1

Brain supplies Goal/Concern identity, Grant, global safety, permission,
resource and priority constraints. It may veto, override, suspend, supersede,
revoke or constrain a local Decision. Decision Governance applies those
constraints to supplied options and owns the local commitment record.

Brain is not required to author every local Decision and Decision Governance
must not bypass a Brain constraint. Final Outcome adjudication and
assimilation remain Brain-owned.
