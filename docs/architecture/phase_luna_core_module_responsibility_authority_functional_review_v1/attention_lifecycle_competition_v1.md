# Attention Lifecycle and Competition v1

## Conceptual lifecycle

`PROPOSED → PRIORITIZED/ALLOCATED → ACTIVE FOCUS → SUSTAINED or DEFERRED →
SATISFIED-CANDIDATE / EXPIRED / SUPERSEDED / RELEASED`.

These are conceptual states only; no new enum is introduced.

A decides Need satisfaction. Attention may expire or release focus based on
time, stale source, budget, target loss or a supplied governance command.
Brain/global policy may preempt or protect focus. Observation reports
acquisition results.

## Competition

Multiple candidates may coexist and compete by relevance, urgency, risk,
uncertainty reduction, cost, modality and protected policy. Attention may rank,
split budgets, retain alternatives and report conflict/starvation. Brain owns
global conflict and resource policy. No global Scheduler is created.

Risk preemption is a candidate/policy application, not an Attention-owned
Safety decision.
