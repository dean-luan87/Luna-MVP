# B-CR / A Authority Boundary

## A owns

- Need, active Hypotheses, evidence relevance, Sufficiency, Reconsideration, and Next-step;
- why B is needed;
- request question, scope, branch/depth/resource constraints within Brain policy;
- B-result adoption, rejection, partial use, retention, or revised request.

## B may decide internally

B may select branch order, expand bounded branch-local alternatives, terminate on limits, mark residual uncertainty, and produce branch-local candidate hypotheses or consequences. These are internal computations/candidates, not canonical Concern-local judgments.

B must never promote branch results into A-active hypotheses, Sufficiency, STOP_SUFFICIENT, final Need, or final continuation.
