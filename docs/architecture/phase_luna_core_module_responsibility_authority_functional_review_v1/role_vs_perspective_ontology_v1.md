# Role versus Perspective Ontology v1

| Concept | Meaning | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| Role | social identity position plus responsibilities, skills, knowledge, obligations and relevance structures | applicable source governance |
| Perspective | Role-conditioned augmented working view | Projection Function/result boundary |
| Persona | stable style/character profile | Personality/Self boundary |
| Identity | subject identity or identity evidence | Self/External Identity source |
| Relationship | relation between subjects | Relationship/Social source |
| Visual viewpoint | camera pose/geometric view | Perception/SLAM source |

Role can be Self, observed-entity, or relationship role. Perspective can be
self-centered, other-centered, organization-centered, task-conditioned, or
another bounded interpretive frame. Emotion is not a current Perspective
conditioner.

Base attributes and relations remain source-owned. Derived attributes are
augmentation candidates; Role-conditioned and Perspective working attributes
must retain provenance and cannot rewrite base world information. Any future
Emotion-related overlay remains deferred until separate adjudication.
