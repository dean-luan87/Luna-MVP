# Role / Perspective / Brain Boundary v1

Brain owns Goal, Concern, Grant, global Safety/Permission/Resource/Priority,
and final adjudication. Brain may constrain Role/Perspective applicability or
request a different projection for a Concern.

Brain must not become the source of every Role identity. Source governance
retains identity evidence and lifecycle. Brain’s global decision may produce a
constraint/ref; it does not directly mutate Role state.
