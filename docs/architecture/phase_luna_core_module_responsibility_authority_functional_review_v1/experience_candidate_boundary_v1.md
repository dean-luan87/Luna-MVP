# ExperienceCandidate Boundary v1

ExperienceCandidate is candidate-only bounded lineage relating cognition,
context, decisions/actions, execution, evidence, and outcomes. It may retain
Role/Perspective and source-version references where necessary for meaning.

It is not Experience, an abstraction/generalization, a success score, a
pattern model, a LearningCandidate execution, or a parameter update. Existing
`ExperienceCandidateV1` and controlled synthetic engine assets are evidence of
a candidate handoff, not a final Experience ontology.

Future path: `ExperienceCandidate → future Experience Governance admission →
admitted Experience`. Admission, generalization, retrieval, and learning are
deferred.
