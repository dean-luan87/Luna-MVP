# A / Brain Semantic Boundary

Brain owns Goal, Concern, grants, global safety/permission/resource/priority and final adjudication/assimilation.

A owns Concern-local reasoning and may return:

- local disposition;
- escalation candidate;
- NewConcernCandidate;
- local outcome/evaluation inputs.

A must not admit/reject/merge/split Concern, issue/revoke global grants, override global constraints, or perform final assimilation. Brain must not duplicate A Need, Hypothesis, Sufficiency, Reconsideration or continuation judgment.

Responsibility follows authority: A owns local reasoning errors; Brain owns global governance errors. A must surface contract violations rather than silently compensate for revoked grants or invalid global constraints.
