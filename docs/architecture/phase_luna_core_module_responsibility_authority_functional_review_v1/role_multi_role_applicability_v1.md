# Multi-Role Applicability v1

Multiple Roles may coexist, for example parent, employee, pedestrian, and
customer. Applicability is scoped by subject, domain, context, validity and
provenance.

Role relations may expose coexistence, conflict, temporary applicability,
dominance candidates, or invalidation candidates. Brain owns global priority
and resource consequences. A judges cognitive consequence. Role sources do
not become a global priority owner.

Role conflict must return an explicit candidate conflict or degraded
applicability state; it must not silently choose the “best” Role.
