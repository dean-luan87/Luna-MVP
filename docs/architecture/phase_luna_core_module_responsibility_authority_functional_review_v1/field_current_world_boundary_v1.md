# Field / Current World Boundary v1

## Distinction

**Field** is the governed operational source-state lineage for environmental
conditions. **Current World** is a cognition-cycle candidate representation
assembled from observations/evidence, Field refs, Context refs and related
uncertainty/conflict/version refs.

Current World is not a writable shadow Field and is not World Truth.

## Legal information directions

There is no single universal one-way chain:

1. `Observation/Evidence → Current World candidate` when the information is an
   observation-local or fused candidate not yet admitted as Field state.
2. `Observation/Evidence → Field Event candidate → Field Event Admission →
   Field Reducer` when the evidence is explicitly eligible for a governed Field
   transition.
3. `Field state/read projection → Current World candidate` when a cognition
   cycle needs the best-known Field state represented in its current-world
   view.

In all cases, Current World cannot directly mutate Field. Field admission and
the reducer retain authority.

## Evidence

`CurrentWorldCandidateV1` is explicitly `candidate_only=True`, carries
`source_versions`, `trace_ref`, `provenance_refs`, uncertainty and conflict
refs, and sets `field_mutation`, `field_transition` and
`field_truth_declaration` false. The B1/context integration similarly creates a
partial or contested Current World candidate without direct Field mutation.

## Boundary rule

Current World may contain candidate attributes, relations and unresolved
conditions derived from evidence. Field may contain admitted operational state.
Neither can declare World Truth. A decides the cognitive significance of a
difference or conflict.

## Legacy terminology risk

`CurrentWorldCandidateV1.active_hypothesis_refs` is a carried ref name, not an
authorization for State Formation or Current World to activate hypotheses.
Active-hypothesis authority remains A. This is a terminology/compatibility
risk, not a reason to change types in this review.
