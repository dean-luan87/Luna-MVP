# Safety / Survival / Minimum Viable Boundary v1

Safety Constitution and Survival Constitution constrain future designs; they
are not runtime controllers or exception mechanisms. The frozen principle is
Safety/Brain policy → preserve minimum viable safe capabilities/resources,
protect critical monitoring, degrade or block nonessential work, and preserve
unknowns/conservative output.

Existing Safety Constitution, Survival Constitution, MONC-like and navigation
guards remain policy/compatibility assets. They are not merged into a new
runtime.
