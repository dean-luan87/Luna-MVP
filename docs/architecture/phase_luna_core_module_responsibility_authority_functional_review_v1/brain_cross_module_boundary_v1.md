# Brain Cross-Module Boundaries

## Brain and A

Brain owns Goal/Concern/grants/global policy. A owns Concern-local Need, hypothesis reasoning, sufficiency, reconsideration, continuation, B request, and B-result evaluation. Brain may constrain or revoke; it must not duplicate local reasoning.

## Brain and Semantic Module

Brain governs which source refs/constraints are admitted into the working envelope. Semantic Module performs construction, expansion, folding, and Working Outline formation. Neither module may silently replace the other.

## Brain and Intent

Intent Governance owns Intent. Brain may govern Goal/Concern/policy and consume Intent refs, but must not mutate Intent directly. Intent→Concern/Task alignment is a governed handoff, not Brain ownership transfer.

## Brain and Role/Field/Context

Brain consumes governed refs and may govern global consequences of changes. Field, Context, and Role/Perspective source owners remain responsible for their state and versions.

## Brain and Attention

Brain sets global resource/safety/priority constraints. A identifies semantic information need. Attention forms priority/budget candidates. Observation/FPO acquires/admit evidence.

## Brain and Capability

Brain may govern global availability, permission, resource, and safety policy. Capability Governance resolves logical capability; Runtime Admission assesses evidence; Provider Governance admits/invokes. Brain never selects model/provider or computes checksums.

## Brain and Loop

Brain may supply accepted high-level lifecycle decisions. Loop validates and persists mechanical commands. Brain does not implement Loop or infer local semantic dispositions.
