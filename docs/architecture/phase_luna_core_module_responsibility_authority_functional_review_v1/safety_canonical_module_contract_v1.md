# Safety Governance — Canonical Contract v1

## Purpose

Safety Governance is the global policy authority constraining cognition and
execution to preserve survival, protected states, non-harm, honesty, consent,
privacy and conservative behavior under uncertainty.

## Owner split

Brain owns global Safety policy, ceilings, protected priorities, override,
revocation and escalation. Safety Governance is the narrow policy/constraint
boundary within Brain's governance plane; Diagnostics, Field, Context,
Observation, Action and Provider supply or enforce local evidence/checks.
