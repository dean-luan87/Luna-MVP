# B-CR Hypothesis and Counterfactual Boundary

## Hypotheses

B may create branch-local hypothesis candidates and alternative branches. A must explicitly adopt, partially use, retain, reject, or supersede them. B cannot promote them into A-authoritative active hypotheses.

## Counterfactuals

B may reason:

- “If X were true…”
- “If evidence Y were invalid…”
- “If route/interpretation Z failed…”
- “If another causal explanation applied…”

Every result must distinguish observed evidence, derived inference, counterfactual branch, and alternative candidate. Counterfactual output cannot enter Current World as fact or World Truth.
