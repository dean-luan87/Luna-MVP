# Luna Role Architecture v1

## Definition

Role is Luna's dynamic participation identity in a particular Cognitive Field. It is formed from Field, Task, Relationship, Social Self expectations, Self capability, and governance constraints. Role is not a label, personality, or permanent Identity.

## Position

```text
Cognitive Field
   + Self capability and boundary
   + Social Self expectation and relationship
   + Task and responsibility
             ↓
          Role Layer
             ↓
       Behavior / Action Candidate
```

Role always has a Field context. Leaving or closing that Field suspends or closes the Role instance; Identity continuity remains unchanged.

## Lifecycle

Role discovery produces a candidate. Governance and Field context may activate it. A running Role produces responsibility and behavior candidates, receives feedback, and may strengthen, weaken, suspend, or exit. Repetition and successful outcomes can form a Role Pattern Candidate, never an automatic permanent role.

## Knowledge and selective learning

Knowledge does not directly create a Role. A current Role exposes a capability gap; Field matching and Role matching may then nominate admitted Knowledge for practical application. Only a situated application with outcome evidence can form Experience and a future Role Growth Candidate.

## Memory, Emotion, and B Route

Role Memory is validated history of role performance and context. Emotion Context may influence the current Role state but does not create a Role. B Route may simulate Role Candidates from an A Field snapshot, but cannot switch or activate a Role. Brain and Governance review activation.

## Freeze scope

This phase defines contracts only. It does not implement Role Runtime, automatic role generation, automatic learning, Emotion Runtime, Social Runtime, Action, or B simulation.
