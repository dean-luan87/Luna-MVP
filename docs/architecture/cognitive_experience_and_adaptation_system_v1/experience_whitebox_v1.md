# Experience and Adaptation Whitebox v1

```text
Outcome
  ↓
Cause Attribution
  ↓
Experience Candidate
  ↓
Experience Evaluation
  ↓
Experience Memory
  ↓
Pattern Candidate
  ↓
Adaptation Candidate
  ↓
Future Attention / Behavior Adjustment
```

## Control questions

- Who creates Experience Candidate? Outcome and Cause Attribution with
  evidence; not a Model directly.
- Who evaluates retention? Experience Evaluation using Repeatability,
  Reliability, Relevance, Impact, and Conflict.
- Who may promote a pattern? A governed retention process; never a single
  unconfirmed event.
- Who wins conflicts? Current Reality; Recent Experience outranks Old
  Experience only as a candidate reference.
- Who owns Goal and final Decision? Brain.
- Who may modify Reality? Reality Reducer after Evidence Gateway validation;
  Experience has no Reality Write authority.
- Who observes adaptation health? Diagnostics, producing a Candidate.

## Isolation invariants

Experience → Attention Pattern Candidate, Experience → Behavior Pattern
Candidate, and Experience → Self Capability Adjustment Candidate are permitted.
Experience → Action, Experience → Goal, Experience → Decision, Experience →
Identity, and Experience → Model Parameter Mutation are forbidden.

## Boundary status

This is a Planning Only artifact. It does not implement automatic learning,
Model Training, parameter updates, Memory Runtime, Emotion Runtime, Role System,
Social Relationship, B Route, Prediction, or Action Runtime.

The single unconfirmed event is not promoted. Old Experience remains explicit.
Diagnostics emits a Diagnostic Candidate. Experience → Behavior Pattern
Candidate and Experience → Identity are governed boundaries.

Experience → Behavior Pattern Candidate is permitted only as a candidate.
