# Cognitive Experience and Adaptation System v1

## Position

Experience is a long-term compression and abstraction of the interaction among
Reality, Field, Self, and Behavior. It is not a storage-only Memory, Reality,
Knowledge, Rule, or Decision. Its only authority is to provide a future
reference candidate.

The governing precedence is:

```text
Current Reality > Recent Experience > Old Experience
```

Current Reality always overrides historical Experience. Experience cannot
overwrite Reality, create a Goal, make a Decision, modify Identity, or execute
Action.

## Core chain

```text
Outcome
  ↓
Cause Attribution
  ↓
Experience Candidate
  ↓
Experience Evaluation
  ↓
Experience Memory
  ↓
Pattern Candidate
  ↓
Adaptation Candidate
  ↓
Future Attention / Behavior Adjustment
```

Every arrow produces a candidate or governed retention state. No online
learning, Model Training, parameter update, automatic policy rewrite, or
automatic action is implemented.

## Experience classes

- Episodic Experience records a bounded concrete event and its Outcome.
- Field Experience records repeated Field context and observed patterns; it is
  not a Field Rule.
- Behavioral Experience records a behavior candidate and Outcome pattern; it
  is not an Action command.
- Self Experience records Capability or Self State changes with evidence; it
  cannot modify Identity.
- Interaction Experience records bounded interaction outcomes and provenance;
  it is not a Social Relationship or Emotion Runtime.

## Evaluation and retention

Experience Value Assessment considers Repeatability, Reliability, Relevance,
Impact, and Conflict. It emits an Experience Retention Candidate. Unknown,
unconfirmed information, one-off noise, and Model guesses are not promoted to
long-term reference without validation.

Retention is bounded by scope, provenance, confidence, recency, decay, and
compression. Episodic detail may be summarized into Field Pattern Candidate,
Behavior Pattern Candidate, Attention Pattern Candidate, or Self Capability
Adjustment Candidate while preserving source and Unknown.

## Adaptation boundary

Adaptation may suggest “Increase attention to exits”, “observe this Field more
carefully”, or “request alternative Capability evidence”. It may not produce
“Always avoid this place”, directly control Action, modify Goal, modify
Decision, change Identity, train a Model, or create an Emotion/Role/Social
state.

## Conflict handling

When Experience conflicts, Current Reality is authoritative. Recent validated
Experience may outweigh Old Experience only as a reference candidate. Conflicts
remain explicit and produce a Conflict Candidate; they are not silently erased.

## Long-term boundary

Experience is designed for later Memory Architecture integration. This phase
does not implement a database, Memory Runtime, automatic learning, Model
Training, Emotion Runtime, Role System, Social Relationship, B Route,
Prediction, or Action Runtime.

Experience is not a Knowledge, not a Rule, and not a Decision. Self Capability
Adjustment Candidate is always a candidate. No online learning. No Emotion
Runtime. No Role System. No Social Relationship. No B Route. No Prediction. No
Action Runtime.
