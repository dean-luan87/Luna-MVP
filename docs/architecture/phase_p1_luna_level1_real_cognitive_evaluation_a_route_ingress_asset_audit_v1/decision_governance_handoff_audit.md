# Decision Governance Handoff Audit

The existing White-box and RF-DETR precedent represent the final cognitive boundary as a handoff to Decision Governance rather than an A-created Decision. `DECISION_GOVERNANCE_HANDOFF` is a White-box node kind; RF-DETR real evidence ended with `next_target=Decision Governance` and no Decision candidate.

Evaluation may observe and archive this handoff ref, its owner, status, trace, provenance, and assertion result. It may not create or admit Decision, Task, or Action.

The A-Route orchestration engine has downstream Decision/Task/Action lifecycle fields, but its controlled runner is synthetic/candidate-only. Those fields do not prove that a real Level-1 cognitive case reaches Decision Governance.

Required future handoff evidence:

- sufficient/stop node produced by A;
- no unresolved required gap;
- handoff ref and owner `Decision Governance`;
- no Decision object created by Evaluation/FPO/Provider;
- trace/profile linkage and failure attribution.
