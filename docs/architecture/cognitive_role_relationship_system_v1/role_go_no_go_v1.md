# Role and Relationship Go / No-Go v1

## Required readiness checks

- Identity ≠ Role is explicit; Role belongs to Field, not Identity.
- Role includes Field Context, Responsibility, Permission, Expectation, Behavior
  Boundary, and Duration.
- Role lifecycle includes Created, Active, Background, Suspended, and Closed.
- Role Activation is Field-driven and emits Role Assignment Candidate.
- Role Conflict Candidate is recorded without automatic resolution.
- Relationship states include Initial, Developing, Stable, Changed, Unknown,
  and Archived.
- Relationship is bound to Participant A, Participant B, Context, History,
  Confidence, Evidence, and Boundary.
- Interaction Experience → Relationship Memory → Relationship Candidate is
  explicit.
- Attention, Behavior, and Emotion interfaces remain candidate/placeholder-only.

## Explicit prohibitions

No Emotion Runtime, No Personality Switching, No Relationship Judgment, No Face
Recognition, No Social Action, No Multi-Agent, No Hive, No B Route, No
Prediction, and No Action Runtime. Role and Relationship cannot directly modify
Identity, Reality, Goal, Decision, or Action.
