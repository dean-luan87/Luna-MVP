# Cognitive Role System Model v1

## Position

Identity ≠ Role. Identity is long-term Self continuity; Role is a Field-bound
social position with responsibility, permission, expectation, behavior boundary,
and duration. Role belongs to Field and is never a personality switch.

```text
Social Field
  ↓
Role Assignment Candidate
  ↓
Relationship Context
  ↓
Social Position
  ↓
Behavior Candidate
  ↓
Emotion Interface
```

Role Assignment Candidate is based on Field Context, Evidence, Confidence,
History, and Unknown. Entering an Office Field may produce Employee Candidate;
entering a Home Field may produce Family Member Candidate. Neither modifies
Identity.

## Role structure and lifecycle

Role contains Field Context, Responsibility, Permission, Expectation, Behavior
Boundary, and Duration. Its lifecycle is:

```text
Created → Active → Background → Suspended → Closed
```

Role Activation is Field-driven. Role Conflict Candidate records simultaneous
or incompatible role contexts without automatically resolving them. Role does
not own Goal, Decision, Social Action, or Emotion.

## Interfaces

Role Context may produce Attention Candidate, Behavior Candidate, Social
Position Candidate, and Emotion State Candidate placeholder. Relationship
Evolution uses Interaction Experience and Relationship Memory to produce a
Relationship Candidate. Current Reality and Evidence remain authoritative.

## Explicit non-goals

No Emotion Runtime, Personality Switching, Relationship Judgment, Face
Recognition, Social Action, Multi-Agent, Hive, B Route, Prediction, or Action
Runtime is implemented.

Behavior Boundary is part of Role. Role does not modify Identity and does not
automatically resolve Role Conflict Candidate. Social Position Candidate is
provisional. No Personality Switching. No Relationship Judgment. No Face
Recognition. No Social Action. No Multi-Agent. No Hive. No B Route. No
Prediction. No Action Runtime.
