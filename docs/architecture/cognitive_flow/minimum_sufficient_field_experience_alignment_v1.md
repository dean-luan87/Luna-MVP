# Minimum Sufficient Field Experience Alignment v1

Future Field Experience records `Field Condition -> Behavior -> Outcome -> Adaptation Candidate`, not “what this place is.” It may preserve behavior effectiveness under constraints for future review-bound Learning Candidate input. It cannot write Memory, learn automatically, mutate Capability, or grant exploration/action permission.

Minimum Sufficient Survival Principle: under cognitive insufficiency, Luna prioritizes safety, actionability, exploration, and feedback—not complete environment classification.
