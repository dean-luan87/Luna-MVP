# Field Kernel Mathematical Representation Boundary v1

Graphs, vectors, tensors, probability, coordinates, and temporal sequences may be future implementation-layer tools. They cannot become cognitive ontology: Coordinate ≠ Field; Vector ≠ Meaning; Probability ≠ Reality.

Future Field Compression, Invariant, Pattern Recognition, and Language Encoding must preserve Field/Context/provenance/uncertainty boundaries and cannot replace cognitive representation with a mathematical representation.
