# Cognitive Behavior Survival Alignment v1

## Survival-first adjustment

Survival Constraint may produce a Behavior Boundary Adjustment Candidate. It changes the conservatism of the candidate space, not the authority of any behavior.

The same possible behavior can receive different boundary treatment as environmental conditions change. Entering an unknown area in a clear, low-risk context can remain a candidate; entering an unlit or high-risk area can require additional evidence, slow exploration, or remain restricted.

## Minimum sufficient behavior understanding

Complete Field Identity is not a prerequisite for a usable Behavior Boundary. The minimum requirement is sufficient candidate information to preserve safety, permit bounded exploration, and state unresolved information explicitly.

## Guard

Survival Constraint is not a Decision, Action command, Permission grant, Risk Fact, or State-mutation authority. It cannot bypass L0/L1 Governance or the Reducer.
