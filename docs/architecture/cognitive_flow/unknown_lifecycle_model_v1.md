# Unknown Lifecycle Model v1

## Lifecycle

```text
Unknown Candidate
      ↓
Open Unknown
      ↓
Resolved / Expired / Conflicted
```

Unknown is a first-class Reality State. The operating space records scope,
reason, evidence gap, impact candidate, confidence, and next observation need.

## Boundary

Elapsed time cannot resolve Unknown automatically. A missing flow speed remains
Unknown until evidence supports a transition. The operating space does not force completion,
fill gaps with prediction, or convert Unknown into a Decision.
