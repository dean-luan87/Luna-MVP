# Decision Candidate Survival Alignment v1

## Survival priority

Survival Constitution remains the highest constraint on candidate eligibility. Decision Candidate Formation can represent risk/reward differences only within the available Survival and Behavior Boundary constraints; it cannot make a survival-exceeding candidate selectable, permitted, or executable.

## Unknown-environment options

Unknown Field can produce Conservative Decision Candidate, Exploration Decision Candidate, Information Seeking Candidate, and Task Deferral Candidate. Unknown is preserved as uncertainty, not treated as failure or filled with an asserted Fact.

`Survival != Risk Avoidance Only`: survival-sensitive candidate space may retain bounded exploration and information seeking, but never automatic risk acceptance or action.
