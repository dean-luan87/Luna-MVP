# Cognitive Commitment Severity Model v1

Commitment Severity Candidate may classify reversible/impact conditions without acting on them:

| Severity candidate | Characteristics | Candidate cognitive requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Low | simple, low impact, reversible | minimum sufficient / fast handling candidate |
| Medium | material cost or uncertainty | evaluation and monitoring candidates |
| High | major impact, low reversibility | additional information/deep reasoning/validation candidates |
| Critical | survival-relevant or potentially irreversible risk | interrupt, minimum-safe, human-assistance, and high-validation candidates |

Severity is not a Decision, Action command, Permission, safety controller, or authority override.
