# Perspective Reality–Emotion Dual Boundary v1

| Layer | Question | Allowed output | Forbidden output |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reality Cognition / A | What is represented as possible, constrained, unknown, or feasible? | Reality, Situation, feasibility candidates | emotional meaning, relationship fact, Action |
| Emotional Cognition | What may this reality mean to this subject and relationship? | meaning, responsibility, care, relationship-weight candidates | Reality truth, uncertainty removal, Action |
| Perspective Context | Which candidate weight/attention bias should Brain consider? | traceable Decision Weight Candidate | Goal/Decision/Action/State mutation |
| Brain | Which candidate trade-off is acceptable under Constitution? | Decision Candidate | direct Action/State mutation |

Reality fact and emotional meaning remain separate. A caring perspective may
increase the visible weight of a child's safety, but it cannot change river
width, capacity, evidence confidence, or feasibility representation.
