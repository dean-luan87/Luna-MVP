# Cognitive Foundation Closure Review v1

## Review findings

The reviewed A-route architecture provides a candidate-first rational cognitive baseline across understanding, attention/resource allocation, A/B routing, reasoning/future-space handling, evaluation/commitment, human execution separation, outcome feedback, and governed experience evolution.

## Closure confirmations

- The complete cognitive lifecycle joins Field observation, understanding, candidates, human/external execution boundary, outcome, experience, and future cognitive adjustment without direct cognition-to-action coupling.
- Present Runtime, past experience/evolution, and future prediction remain distinct.
- The truth hierarchy preserves the difference between current evidence, knowledge, experience, hypotheses, predictions, and culture.
- Failure paths support local re-observation, reconstruction, revision, and applicability adjustment without historical erasure or foundational rewrite.
- Emotion, World Model, Language, Hive, and Embodiment are extension-only interfaces subject to local candidate validation.

## Remaining governance status

This is an architecture closure review, not final Phase GO. Runtime implementation, model integration, Memory/Hive mutation, execution authority, Emotion, Identity, social relationship, Language internalization, World Model, and Hive evolution remain outside this phase.
