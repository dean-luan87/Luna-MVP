# Deep Reasoning Activation Condition Model v1

Activation Condition Candidate may be supported by high survival impact, high risk, high uncertainty, high goal/mission value, material future-branch conflict, insufficient pattern match, information value, or an available cognitive time/resource window.

Deep Reasoning is not Always Better. Longer Reasoning is not Correctness. Low-risk or time-constrained contexts may retain A-route pattern match, reactive, or bounded exploration candidates.
