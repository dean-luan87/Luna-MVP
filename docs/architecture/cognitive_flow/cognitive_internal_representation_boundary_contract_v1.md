- Internal Representation != Knowledge.
- Internal Representation != Memory.

- Internal Representation != Truth.
- Internal Representation != Embedding.
- Internal Representation != Vector Database.
- Representation != Decision.
- Representation != Action.
- Representation != Permission.
- Pattern != Rule.
- Pattern != Truth.
- Unknown != Failure.

- Simulation != Prediction Truth.

Representation candidate use remains context-bound, source-preserving, locally validated, and subject to Governance. No representation directly mutates Field/Reducer/Memory/Experience State, Genome, Culture, or Gene Pool.
