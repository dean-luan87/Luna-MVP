# Decision Delay Model v1

## Delay candidate

Decision Delay Candidate expresses that the present option basis should remain unresolved pending more information, changed context, reduced conflict, improved resource availability, or human assistance. Delay is a cognitive-path candidate, not inaction, refusal, or an action command.

## Conditions

Delay may be relevant when evidence is insufficient, risks are poorly understood, candidate conflicts are material, information value is high, time permits, or a safer alternative remains open. It may coexist with information-seeking, conservative, or alternative-path candidates.

Delay does not grant a wait action, suspend an external system, or freeze Field/State. It records only a future-decision precondition.
