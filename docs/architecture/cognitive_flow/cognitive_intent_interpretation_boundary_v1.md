# Cognitive Intent Interpretation Boundary v1

## Layered interpretation

| Layer | Permitted interpretation | Must preserve | Must not do |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brain | establishes Original Purpose and cognitive success concern | self/goal/constitution context | delegate final judgment to a model |
| Neural Governance | organizes information coverage and resource-bounded path candidates | purpose, non-negotiable objective, evidence need | remove the objective due to convenience |
| Attention Axis | forms observation needs and priority candidates | required information meaning | turn observation into an Action |
| Middleware / Capability | explains feasible capability organization | requirement boundary and constraints | redefine the cognitive objective or substitute model metrics |
| Provider | returns bounded local output | scope/provenance/uncertainty | claim cognitive completion or Truth |
| Evidence / Reality Cognition | evaluates evidence support and situation impact candidates | intent trace lineage | equate output availability with intent satisfaction |

## Rule

Every interpretation must retain `intent_ref`, identify its permitted
interpretation range, and record any constraint that prevents full coverage.
Resource limitations may produce a constrained candidate or gap; they cannot silently change the Original Purpose.
