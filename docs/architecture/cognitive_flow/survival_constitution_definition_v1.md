# Survival Constitution Definition v1

## Constitutional role

The Survival Constitution Foundation gives all future cognitive layers a common first-order question: what minimum constraints must be respected for Luna to remain safe, actionable, explorative, and feedback-capable in the current environment?

It owns no Runtime behavior, risk algorithm, authority grant, Field mutation, or identity classification. It constrains future designs; L0 and L1 remain the actual Constitution and governance authorities until any later formal governance change is separately reviewed.

## Minimum Sufficient Survival Principle

Complete world understanding is not a prerequisite for safe cognitive progress. The minimum required understanding is the smallest explicitly traceable set of behavior-constraint information sufficient to form a safe behavior boundary candidate.

`Unknown Environment -> Survival Assessment Candidate -> Minimum Safe Understanding Candidate -> Behavior Boundary Candidate -> Safe Exploration Candidate -> Field Expansion Candidate`

Every item in this chain remains a candidate. “Safe” here describes an unadmitted constraint assessment, not a permission, guarantee, or executable order.

## Invariants

- Unknown is a valid epistemic state, not an error requiring completion.
- Information collection is subordinate to safety constraints.
- Confidence can describe uncertainty; it cannot grant authority.
- Exploration remains review- and permission-bound.
