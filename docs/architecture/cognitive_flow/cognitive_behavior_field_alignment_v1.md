# Cognitive Behavior Field Alignment v1

## Field-to-behavior alignment

Field Identity answers “what kind of Field is this?” Field Affordance answers “what patterns may this Field support or constrain?” Behavior Boundary answers “what behavior may Luna currently consider under present Context, Survival, Belief, and Goal?”

For example, a public square may afford passage, stopping, and social activity. At night, during maintenance, and with crowd uncertainty, the current Behavior Boundary may retain slow observation and safe passage as candidates while restricting rapid traversal or entry into an unknown area. The stable Field Identity is not rewritten by this temporary boundary.

## Unknown Field rule

Unknown Field does not mean no behavior. It produces a reduced behavior space: safe stop, slow movement, observation, distance preservation, and information acquisition can remain candidates. Fast entry, broad movement, and unknown-object interaction may remain restricted or unknown until evidence changes.

## Preserved boundary

Field Affordance is not user intent, current permission, Decision, or Action. Behavior Boundary does not mutate Field, Field View, Field State, Snapshot, or Context.
