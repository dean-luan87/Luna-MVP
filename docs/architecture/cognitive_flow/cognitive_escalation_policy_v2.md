# Cognitive Escalation Policy v2

## Escalation triggers

Neural or Diagnostics may form an Escalation Candidate for:

- risk increase or survival impact;
- unknown growth or low confidence;
- evidence conflict or Reality State conflict;
- capability degradation or hardware failure;
- resource stress or envelope violation;
- temporal change affecting an active process;
- process authority or intent preservation concern.

## Escalation levels

1. **Local Regulation Candidate:** bounded monitoring or resource adjustment
   candidate within existing policy;
2. **Neural Review Candidate:** requires Neural coordination across processes or
   capability/resource governance;
3. **Brain Review Candidate:** affects Situation, Goal context, Decision
   confidence, Self Capability Awareness, or survival priority.

Escalation levels do not grant Action authority. A Hardware Manager finding that a
camera is degraded emits a capability state candidate with reason and confidence;
it cannot decide to stop observing or change Luna's life goals.
