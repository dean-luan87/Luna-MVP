# Luna Cognitive Concept Layer Evolution Strategy v1

Concept types, patterns, compression vocabulary, and contextual/domain variants may evolve through future Learning Governance, Environment evidence, Experience, or Hive Knowledge references. They may not automatically learn, modify their own schema, rewrite Luna Core, or write Memory.

All evolution must preserve candidate semantics, primitive/evidence/context lineage, uncertainty, trace, and Field/Reducer boundaries. Experience/Hive input is future review-bound reference material, not a Concept authority. High-impact changes to Concept meaning, compression level, Primitive mapping, or consumer compatibility require separate Protocol/Governance review and migration evidence.

Language remains an output expression layer. It may render governed Concept/Meaning representation but cannot become the producer, classifier, or authority for internal Concept structure.
