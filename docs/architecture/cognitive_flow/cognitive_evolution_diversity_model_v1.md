# Cognitive Evolution Diversity Model v1

Cognitive Diversity Candidate preserves different cognitive ecologies and niches. A navigation-oriented individual may emphasize spatial pattern, risk, and route; a companionship-oriented individual may emphasize relationship and future emotion-interface; an enterprise-oriented individual may emphasize process and organization patterns.

Difference is not Error. Diversity cannot be collapsed by central normalization, hive override, or automatic population synchronization.
