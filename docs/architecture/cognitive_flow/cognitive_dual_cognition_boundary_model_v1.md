# Reality / Emotional Cognition Boundary v1

## Complementary cognitive systems

| System | Primary question | Inputs | Output boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reality Cognition | What is represented in the physical world, and how does it relate to capability and feasibility? | evidence, space, capability, resources, causal candidates | Reality, Situation, and feasibility candidates |
| Emotional Cognition | What personal, relational, or value meaning may this have? | relationship, value, identity, meaning-bearing experience | affective and relational candidates |

They may share Identity and Experience references, but their inference
criteria, output purposes, and authority are isolated.

- Emotional Cognition cannot convert emotional interpretation into Reality
  truth, remove a Reality uncertainty, or mutate World State.
- Reality Cognition does not declare personality, emotion, or personal meaning.
- Neither system owns Decision, Action, or State mutation in this phase.
