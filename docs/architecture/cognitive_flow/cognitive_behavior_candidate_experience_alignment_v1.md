# Cognitive Behavior Candidate Experience Alignment v1

## Historical pattern input

Experience may contribute a traceable historical behavior-pattern reference: for example, similar industrial Fields may have used protected walkways. It can add a candidate such as “seek the designated walkway,” together with provenance and current-evidence requirements.

## No shortcut rule

Current Context, Field, Survival Constraint, and Behavior Boundary continue to govern every candidate. Experience cannot make a pattern current Reality, Permission, Decision, or automatic selection. Historical patterns remain candidate evidence and may be contradicted by current Field information.

`Experience != Behavior Permission` and `Experience != Decision`.
