# Cognitive Population Hive Boundary v1

Future Hive may retain Validated Cognitive Pattern Candidates, Historical Patterns, Experimental Patterns, or Deprecated Pattern Candidates. Hive is not a central brain, individual controller, reality replacement, local decision authority, or individual-experience override.

Individual experience cannot directly overwrite population patterns; population patterns cannot directly overwrite individual local experience or representation. All future exchange remains Candidate -> Validation -> Adaptation Candidate.
