# Decision Resolution Value Alignment v1

## Comparison candidate

Value Comparison Candidate compares remaining option descriptions across survival impact, Mission Alignment, goal contribution, information gain, resource cost, risk exposure, opportunity cost, and reversibility. It retains dimensions rather than declaring a universal best score.

## Risk/reward boundary

Survival does not mean zero-risk avoidance. Within current Survival and Behavior Boundary conditions, a candidate may describe bounded risk as potentially relevant to survival opportunity, goal, or information value. The comparison cannot make a risk a Fact, convert value into truth, or choose a behavior.

`Value != Truth`, `Value != Decision`, and `Risk != Fact`.
