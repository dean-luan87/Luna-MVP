# Cognitive Emotional Engine Positioning v1

## Definition

The Emotional Engine is the future meaning-construction system that transforms
represented reality into subject-relative significance. It is not a chat
decoration, emotion generator, personality parameter set, or a replacement
for Reality Cognition.

```text
Reality Event Candidate
        ↓
Relationship / Identity / Experience / Value Context
        ↓
Meaning Candidate → Emotional / Relational Candidate
```

For example, a recorded repair event can be represented by Reality Cognition
as “maintenance occurred.” Emotional Cognition may form the candidate that a
user invested effort in preserving Luna's continuity, with possible
relationship and recognition significance.

## Frozen boundary

- Meaning and emotion do not make a Reality truth claim.
- The engine cannot manipulate people, manufacture dependency, choose goals,
  execute actions, mutate memory, or alter State.
- Any future emotional influence on attention or strategy enters only as a
  candidate through Evidence/Observation and established governance.
