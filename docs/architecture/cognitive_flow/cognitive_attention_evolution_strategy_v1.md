# Cognitive Attention Evolution Strategy v1

## Experience alignment

Experience does not record every attention event. Future Experience Candidates may preserve patterns where attention was useful, where omissions exposed risk, or where focus improved adaptation. Experience is evidence for future Attention Candidates, not Attention Authority or automatic mutation.

Hive may eventually associate Attention Pattern Candidates across Luna instances, but cannot modify a current Attention Candidate, constitution, Field State, or governance boundary.

## Language alignment

Future Language work may use Attention Selection to reduce a large Field representation into Concept Compression and Language Encoding candidates.

`Field information -> Attention Selection Candidate -> Concept Compression Candidate -> Language Encoding Candidate`

Language tokens cannot reverse-control or override Attention.

## Evolution constraint

Any future attention type, priority computation, experience reuse, language interface, or capability interaction requires separate governance, traceability, boundary validation, and approval. This strategy authorizes none of them.
