# Decision Resolution Experience Alignment v1

## Permitted influence

Experience/Library may provide Similar Case, historical outcome, risk pattern, reversibility pattern, or comparison-structure candidates. They must preserve provenance, uncertainty, and current-context dependency.

## Prohibited influence

Experience cannot bypass candidate filtering, replace current Field/Context evidence, resolve a conflict automatically, choose an option, commit a Decision, grant Permission, establish truth, or change State.

`Experience != Shortcut`, `Experience != Decision Authority`, and `Knowledge != Permission`.
