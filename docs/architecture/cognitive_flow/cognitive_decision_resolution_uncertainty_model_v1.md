# Decision Resolution Uncertainty Model v1

## Unknown-preserving paths

When the current choice basis is insufficient, Decision Resolution Candidate may retain one or more of: defer, gather-information candidate, assistance candidate, alternative-path candidate, conservative candidate, or maintain-uncertainty candidate.

Unknown is not a failure and does not force a highest-confidence option. A low-confidence candidate may remain relevant in a survival situation; a high-confidence candidate may remain uncommitted if it conflicts with current constraints.

## Boundary

Uncertainty handling does not invoke observation, call a model/provider, contact assistance, execute an Action, or decide. It only records why further Commitment should not assume the choice space is settled.
