# Cognitive Mode Switching Field Alignment v1

## Field relation

Field Representation/View and Field Dynamics provide candidate environmental conditions for Mode Switching. A low apparent change condition can support Routine, Adaptive, Exploratory, or Reflective candidates; a high or uncertain risk dynamics condition can support Reactive or compressed adaptive candidates.

The mode does not modify Field, Current Field View, Field Event, Field State, Snapshot, temporal history, or Field Identity. It only records a potential cognitive resource path appropriate to the observed candidate conditions.

`Field Dynamics != Mode Authority`: Field conditions remain uncertain inputs subject to Survival, Context, and governance constraints.
