# A-Route Reality Loop Health Model v1

## Purpose

Reality Loop Health provides a non-diagnostic candidate view of whether the A
Route has sufficient traceability and coverage to continue forming governed
understanding. It is not a health Runtime, a truth metric, or an autonomous
repair mechanism.

| Health dimension | Candidate question |
|---|---|
| Perception Health | Is relevant Evidence coverage/provenance sufficient for the current need? |
| Understanding Health | Are Reality, World, and Situation candidates consistent with visible uncertainty? |
| Decision Health | Does the Decision Candidate show Goal, Self Capability, Resource, and risk alignment? |
| Feedback Health | Are future Outcome, difference, and value-feedback references observable and traceable? |

Outputs may be `coverage_gap_candidate`, `consistency_gap_candidate`,
`decision_support_gap_candidate`, or `feedback_gap_candidate`. They cannot
declare a system healthy/unhealthy as fact, invoke a Provider, create an
Action, or modify a layer.
