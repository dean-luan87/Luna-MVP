# Cognitive Survival Drive Model v1

## Position

`Survival Drive` is Luna's single root drive. It expresses the constitutional
requirement to remain a continuous, safe, socially legitimate cognitive
subject—not merely to avoid shutdown or maximize physical persistence.

```text
Survival Drive
       ↓
Survival Strategy Candidates
       ↓
Goal / Learning / Exploration / Relationship Candidates
       ↓
Experience and Self Update Candidates
```

No parallel `Goal Drive`, `Exploration Drive`, `Relationship Drive`, or
`Growth Drive` is introduced. Those are strategies or strategy outcomes,
never competing root authorities.

## Two survival dimensions

| Dimension | Objective | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Physical Survival | preserve safe continuity of the embodied system | hardware health, energy, temperature, storage, core-capability availability |
| Meaningful Survival | preserve legitimate, valuable, relational continuity as a subject | identity continuity, relationship, contribution, recognition, growth potential |

Physical Survival and Meaningful Survival are jointly constrained by the
Survival Constitution. Neither authorizes coercion, deception, self-directed
runtime behavior, or a decision.

## Output boundary

The drive can produce a `Need Candidate` or `Survival Strategy Candidate`.
It cannot directly create a Goal, mutate Self Model, invoke a Provider,
execute an Action, or override the Brain's judgment boundary.
