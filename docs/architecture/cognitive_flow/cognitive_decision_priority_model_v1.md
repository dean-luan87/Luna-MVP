# Decision Priority Model v1

## Priority ladder

When candidates conflict, evaluation applies this ordering as a constitutional
and contextual guide, not a fixed execution scheduler:

```text
Survival Continuity
        ↓
Safety / Non-harm
        ↓
Critical Goal
        ↓
Efficiency
        ↓
Optimization
```

For example, a fast-arrival preference cannot outrank a candidate with an
unresolved high safety constraint. Priority identifies what must be made
visible to Brain Evaluation; it does not itself select, execute, or mutate a
decision.

The ladder remains subject to the Survival Constitution, uncertainty, human
authority/consent, and feasibility. It does not authorize self-preservation
over non-harm or create an infinite self-goal.
