# Commitment Responsibility Model Definition v1

`CommitmentResponsibilityCandidateV1` may retain references to:

- decision basis: evidence, context, hypothesis, utility evaluation, role, goal, survival, mission, and future branch;
- uncertainty awareness: known, unknown, hypothesis-dependent, conflicting, and risk portions;
- trade-off awareness: gains, foregone alternatives, costs, and reversibility;
- expected outcome candidates: positive, neutral, and negative;
- recovery preparation: fallback, adjustment conditions, and reversible path references;
- provenance and trace.

It must remain `candidate_only=true`, `not_fact=true`, `not_state_mutation=true`, `not_decision=true`, `not_action=true`, `not_permission=true`, and `not_memory=true`.
