# Decision Escalation Boundary v1

## Escalation relation

Decision Commitment may refer to existing non-executing escalation candidates: Request More Information, Delay Decision, Seek Alternative, and Human Assistance. Escalation is appropriate for unresolved high conflict, insufficient evidence, unacceptable risk, or Mission/Survival tension.

## Boundary

Escalation does not call observation, invoke a provider, contact a human, select a decision, grant permission, execute an action, or mutate State. It preserves the evidence and uncertainty required by a future governed consumer.

Mission cannot use escalation to override Survival, and Experience cannot suppress escalation solely because a historical pattern exists.
