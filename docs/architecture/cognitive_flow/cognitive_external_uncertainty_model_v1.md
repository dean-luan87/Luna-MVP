# Cognitive External Uncertainty Model v1

External uncertainty is first-class candidate information, not a defect hidden by an adapter.

It may include:

- recognition ambiguity;
- source/model limitation;
- stale time window;
- spatial ambiguity or SLAM drift candidate;
- OCR/ASR transcription ambiguity;
- VLM alternative scene interpretations; and
- missing or contradicting evidence.

Uncertainty Candidate may influence Attention, Sufficiency, or a future Routing Candidate through evaluation. It cannot directly generate a Decision, Action, Permission, or Reality change.
