# Cognitive Architecture Boundary Review v1

## Review result

The A-route candidate flow is complete from Observation through Understanding, Evaluation, Candidate Formation, Outcome, Experience, Compression, Representation Evolution, and Population/Culture candidates.

## Reducer boundary

All Cognitive Flow modules may:

- Generate Candidate;
- Update Understanding Candidate;
- Provide Evidence;
- Provide Evaluation.

They may not:

- Direct State Mutation;
- Direct Decision Execution;
- Direct Permission Grant.

Reducer remains the only State Mutation Authority.

## Coupling review

The architecture maintains separation between experience and rule, culture and truth, gene pool and authority, optimization and decision, prediction and reality, and future emotion/world-model/hive interfaces and their downstream authority.
