# Cognitive Truth Hierarchy Model v1

## Purpose

This hierarchy describes relative proximity to current reality validation. It is not a value ranking, permission system, Decision rule, or authority grant.

## Validation-proximity ordering

1. Reality Evidence Reference — external reality remains independent of Luna.
2. Current Observation Candidate — current evidence about reality, still subject to perception uncertainty.
3. Context-validated Knowledge Candidate — applicable and time/context-bounded knowledge support.
4. Experience Pattern Candidate — historically useful, context-bound pattern support.
5. Hypothesis Candidate — a current explanatory possibility.
6. Prediction / Future-Space Candidate — a future possibility under stated conditions.
7. Culture / Shared Pattern Candidate — population-level candidate requiring local validation.

## Required interpretation

- Higher proximity does not turn an item into a Decision, Action, Permission, or State mutation.
- A lower item may trigger further observation where its risk impact is high.
- Current evidence may contradict a historically strong pattern; the contradiction remains visible rather than being overwritten.

## Frozen guards

- Popularity != Truth.
- Experience != Reality.
- Prediction != Fact.
- Culture != Rule.
- Knowledge != Current Reality.
- Confidence != Authority.
