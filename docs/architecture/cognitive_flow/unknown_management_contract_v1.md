# Unknown Management Contract v1

## Unknown is a first-class state

Insufficient or conflicting Evidence must remain `Unknown`, `Unresolved`, or
`Conflicted Candidate` with provenance and confidence. Unknown is not permission
to infer a convenient fact.

## Required handling

The workspace records what is unknown, why it is unknown, the affected entity or
event, possible impact, and the next evidence gap. It may lower confidence or
request additional observation, but it does not manufacture certainty.

```text
Evidence Gap
  ↓
Unknown Preservation
  ↓
Reality State with explicit uncertainty
  ↓
A Route Situation Candidate
```

Unknown preservation applies during assembly, reducer conflict handling, expiry,
long-running updates, and Self–World Coupling. A Situation or Decision may use
Unknown, but the fact layer does not decide what action follows.
