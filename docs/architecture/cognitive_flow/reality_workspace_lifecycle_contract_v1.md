# Reality Workspace Lifecycle Contract v1

## State lifecycles

Reality Workspace retains representations by utility and validity rather than by
raw observation volume:

```text
Candidate
  ↓
Active
  ↓
Stale
  ↓
Expired
```

The lifecycle applies independently to entities, events, relations, states, and
Self Capability observations. A withdrawn or contradicted candidate may be
invalidated without becoming a fact.

## Retention tiers

- **Immediate State:** seconds; current transient observation and safety-relevant
  changes;
- **Working Reality:** minutes to hours; current room, route, task, and active
  context;
- **Stable Reality Pattern:** long-lived validated pattern, retained only when
  repeated, relevant, or survival-significant.

## Compression boundary

Reality Compression preserves important change, relationship change,
survival-relevant information, repeated validated patterns, provenance, and
Unknown impact. It may discard redundant low-value slices. Compression is not
automatic learning and does not replace current Evidence with memory.
