# Cognitive Observation Attention Selection Model v1

Future selection candidates may express six priority dimensions: Survival Priority, Risk Priority, Task Priority, Uncertainty Reduction Priority, Change Detection Priority, and Experience Relevance Priority.

Dimensions are traceable candidate considerations, not a single confidence score, a deterministic action policy, or authority. Priority may identify which Observation Need Candidate deserves further cognitive work; it cannot execute the observation or bypass L1 governance.

The planned output preserves Context, Observation Need, Survival, Goal, Field, Information Gap, provenance, trace, and candidate-only boundary flags.
