# Decision Candidate Mission Alignment v1

## Mission influence

Mission Constraint can contribute a Mission Alignment reference to a Decision Candidate and influence candidate value interpretation. It cannot become a command, Action Permission, or Survival override.

Where Mission Alignment is high and survival risk exceeds the boundary, the candidate must retain the conflict and survival constraint. Formation may represent a delay, alternative path, more-information, or assistance candidate, but cannot resolve the conflict by selecting an unsafe option.

Mission remains distinct from Goal and Task: it contextualizes higher-order value without replacing current goal admission or future Decision governance.
