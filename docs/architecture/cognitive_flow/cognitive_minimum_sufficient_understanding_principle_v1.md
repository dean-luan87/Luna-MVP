# Cognitive Minimum Sufficient Understanding Principle v1

Minimum Sufficient Understanding is the smallest governed understanding candidate that supports the next safe, task-relevant cognitive stage under current Survival, Risk, Time, Resource, and Information Value constraints.

Unknown is valid when its impact is low. High-impact unknowns may support observation, exploration, reasoning, deferral, or routing candidates. Sufficiency is not Truth, Confidence, Permission, Decision, or Action.

Minimum Sufficient Information is an A-Route-wide information organization
principle. Available Cognitive Information is not automatically Active Cognitive
Information: governed relevance selection may form a minimum relevant candidate
view while excluded or dormant information remains recoverable. Self and
External information use the same selection logic; Self-specific first-person,
capability/resource, and authority boundaries remain with their existing
owners.
