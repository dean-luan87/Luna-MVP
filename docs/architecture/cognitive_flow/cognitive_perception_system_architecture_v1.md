# Perception and Situation Understanding System Architecture v1

## Purpose

Answers: what may Luna have observed, and what Field/Situation may it be in?

## Existing module grouping

Field Observation, Primitive Understanding, Concept Formation, Field Representation/View, Relationship Candidate, Situation Understanding, and Affordance Understanding.

## Input and output

Inputs are governed Field/Observation, primitive/concept, relation, temporal/spatial, and Context references. Outputs are Field Understanding, Situation, Relationship, and Affordance candidates.

## Boundary

The system does not determine Goal, Decision, Action, Permission, Fact, or State. Observation is not Fact and Situation is not Context.
