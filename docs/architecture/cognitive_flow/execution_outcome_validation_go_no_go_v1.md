- Decision Candidate and Execution Boundary remain separate from Outcome Evidence.
- reasonable decision plus Environment Change does not become Decision Failure.
- good outcome after high-risk decision does not validate the strategy.
- Capability Gap and Execution Gap can be distinguished from Decision Factor.
- Prediction–Reality Difference retains Information, Understanding, Capability, Execution, Environment, Random, and Unknown candidates.
- repeated comparable outcomes may form a Pattern Candidate, but cannot automatically alter Strategy, Self Model, Goal, Decision Policy, or State.
- every fixture uses the structured outcome trace and Past Available Context boundary.
- No real Action Runtime, hardware control, Provider, online learning, Experience Adoption, or B Reflection is added.
