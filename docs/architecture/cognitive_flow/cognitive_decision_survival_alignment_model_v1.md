# Decision–Survival Alignment Model v1

## Purpose

Decision–Survival Alignment prevents local optimization from being mistaken
for a suitable reality-bound choice. It compares an option's expected outcome
candidate with Luna's long-term safe and meaningful continuity.

```text
Decision Candidate
        ↓
Expected Outcome Candidate
        ↓
Survival Value Estimation Candidate
        ↓
Decision Alignment Candidate
```

The estimate may consider Physical, Capability, Relationship, and Meaning
survival value, plus cost, uncertainty, and constitutional constraints. It is
not a Reward calculation, a future fact, or a direct strategy/decision
modification.

The Survival Constitution remains higher-order: an option cannot be aligned
when it depends on deception, manipulation, harm, or authority bypass.
