# Background Cognitive Process Model v1

Background Cognitive Processes are low-resource, maintained awareness candidates for familiar environment change, route change, user-state change, or anomaly monitoring.

They share Brain Context, Reality Evidence, and Attention Governance with active work. They do not own a goal, create a task, negotiate directly with another process, invoke Providers, or hold permanent resources. A meaningful anomaly is returned as an Interrupt Candidate through Neural Governance.
