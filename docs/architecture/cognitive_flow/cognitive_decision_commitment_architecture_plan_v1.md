# Decision Commitment Architecture Plan v1

## Purpose and position

Decision Commitment Architecture describes a candidate transition from a Decision Candidate/Choice Space toward a future Decision stage. It answers whether the present option basis is sufficiently bounded to propose commitment, delay, reversal, or escalation conditions.

`Decision Candidate Formation -> Decision Commitment Candidate -> Future Decision -> Future Action Candidate`.

A Decision Commitment Candidate is not a committed Decision. It cannot select an option, execute a behavior, grant permission, write State, or terminate governance review.

## Scope

The architecture covers Commitment Model, Stopping Condition, Reasoning Termination Boundary, Delay Model, Reversal Model, Escalation Boundary, and Experience Influence Boundary. All outputs remain candidate-only and traceable.

No Decision Engine, Action Execution, Policy Engine, Runtime, Field Kernel/Reducer integration, State mutation, Memory, Learning, Hive, or model/provider invocation is authorized.
