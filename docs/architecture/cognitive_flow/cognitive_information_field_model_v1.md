# Cognitive Information Field Model v1

## Reality Field and Information Field

Reality Field describes current external world candidates with temporal/spatial specificity and evidence constraints. Cognitive Information Field describes heterogeneous information candidates being processed: they may concern past, present, future, abstraction, uncertainty, or hypothesis.

Example: a person at home planning Tokyo travel may have current Reality Field references to home, while the Information Field holds Tokyo map, flight, hotel, travel experience, cultural knowledge, and preference candidates.

Cognitive Information Field != Reality. It cannot replace Reality Field, confirm events, or mutate State.
