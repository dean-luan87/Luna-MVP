# Commitment Responsibility Boundary Contract v1

## Allowed candidate references

Governed Decision Commitment, Decision Candidate, Optimization, Future Space and Future Branch, Context, Role and Goal, Survival and Mission, Evidence and Hypothesis, Outcome and Experience, provenance, and trace references may be read.

## Rejected inputs

Raw model/provider output, Fact Store, State handle, Reducer command, Decision result, Action command, Permission, Memory, Learning execution, and Hive authority are outside this layer.

## Output boundary

Outputs are candidate-only, not fact, not state mutation, not decision, not action, not permission, and not memory.

## Negative guards

- Commitment Responsibility != Decision.
- Responsibility != Blame.
- Outcome != Truth.
- Confidence != Authority.
- Experience != Automatic Correction.
- Failure != Learning Trigger.
- Commitment != Future Truth.
- Highest Utility != Correct Decision.
- Optimal Candidate != Guaranteed Result.
- Reducer != Commitment Manager.
- Reducer remains the only State Mutation Authority.
