# Cognitive Risk Reward Balance Model v1

## Candidate balance

Risk/reward balance is a multi-dimensional candidate comparison, not a reward model or reinforcement-learning signal. It preserves potential upside, risk exposure, uncertainty, reversibility, resource cost, survival impact, Mission Alignment, and goal contribution without collapsing them into an authoritative result.

## Life-like alignment

- In lower-risk conditions, low-cost exploration may remain a candidate.
- In higher-risk conditions, Cognitive Constraint can limit depth and preserve faster, bounded response candidates.
- When information value is low, accepting unknown can remain a valid candidate path.
- When information value is high and resources permit, further information acquisition can remain a candidate path.

No condition chooses, permits, or executes a behavior. `Risk != Fact`, and a favorable reward description cannot override Survival or Behavior Boundary.
