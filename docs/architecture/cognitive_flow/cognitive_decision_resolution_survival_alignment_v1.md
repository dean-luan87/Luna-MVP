# Decision Resolution Survival Alignment v1

## Survival first

Survival Constitution is the highest constraint on the Resolution Candidate. Resolution may compare risky possibilities only in the candidate space that current Survival, Cognitive Constraint, and Behavior Boundary allow. It cannot use Mission, value, confidence, urgency, or Experience to exceed Survival constraints.

## Survival opportunity

Survival alignment is not an instruction to be passive. Under acceptable candidate boundaries, it can preserve exploration, information seeking, deferral, avoidance, or assistance paths that may improve continued safe operation. No path is selected or executed here.

`Mission != Survival Override` and `Confidence != Authority`.
