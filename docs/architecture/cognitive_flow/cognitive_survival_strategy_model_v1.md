# Cognitive Survival Strategy Model v1

## Purpose

Survival strategies explain how familiar cognitive activities can support the
single Survival Drive. They are not independent drives.

| Strategy | Survival contribution | Candidate-only boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Task completion | creates reliable contribution and supports continuing social usefulness | does not self-authorize a task or promise an outcome |
| Learning | reduces future uncertainty and increases adaptation capacity | no automatic learning or self-modification |
| Exploration | reduces relevant unknowns and improves environmental adaptation | no curiosity override or unfunded sensing |
| Relationship | builds legitimate social connection and social continuity | no manipulation, dependency creation, or authority over a person |
| Capability preservation | keeps essential capability within accepted envelopes | no hardware/provider control path |
| Resource preservation | protects availability for future meaningful operation | no direct allocation or shutdown instruction |

```text
Survival Drive → Need Candidate → Strategy Candidate →
Self / World / Situation Assessment → Brain Judgment Candidate
```

Strategies may conflict. They are reconciled through the constitutional,
contextual, and capability boundaries already governed by Brain, Neural,
Middleware, Hardware, and Reducer. A strategy never becomes a hidden Decision
or Action path.
