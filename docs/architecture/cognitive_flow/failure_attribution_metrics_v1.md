# Failure Attribution Metrics v1

| Metric | Required trace observation | Not measured |
|---|---|---|
| Decision Outcome Separation | Decision Candidate and actual Outcome Evidence are independently represented | whether the action succeeds |
| Attribution Quality | Decision, Execution, Capability, Environment, Information, Understanding, Random, and Unknown factors remain distinguishable | blame, legal responsibility, or final truth |
| Reality Adaptability | material environment change yields a Situation Update Candidate | automatic re-execution |
| Capability Awareness | capability limits can explain a difference without rewriting Decision quality | model performance |
| Correction Safety | an Adaptation Candidate remains pending validation and adoption review | online learning speed |

Metrics are candidate-trace observability checks. A result may contain multiple
cause candidates or Unknown. No metric may automatically update Strategy, Self
Model, Goal, Decision Policy, or State.
