# Cognitive Depth Regulation Cognitive Flow Alignment v1

Attention answers “what deserves focus?” Depth answers “after focus, how much cognitive expansion is appropriate?” They are distinct, independently traceable candidates.

`Unknown -> Survival Assessment Candidate -> Depth Regulation Candidate -> Information Strategy Candidate`

Unknown does not imply Deep Reasoning. Under high pressure and short time, a future Reactive Candidate may be appropriate; under low risk and high information value, Exploration may be appropriate. In neither case does Depth create Decision, Action, Fact, Permission, Field State, or Reducer command.
