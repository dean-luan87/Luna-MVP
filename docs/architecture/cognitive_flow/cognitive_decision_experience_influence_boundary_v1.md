# Decision Experience Influence Boundary v1

## Permitted influence

Experience/Library may provide traceable Similar Case Candidates, pattern references, expected-condition references, or commitment-structure candidates. They can accelerate evaluation only by contributing explicit evidence and uncertainty references.

## Prohibited influence

Experience cannot directly commit a Decision Candidate, skip stopping/delay/escalation conditions, select an option, grant permission, establish truth, modify State, or override Survival/Mission/Context constraints.

`Experience != Automatic Commitment`, `Experience != Decision Authority`, and `Knowledge != Permission`.
