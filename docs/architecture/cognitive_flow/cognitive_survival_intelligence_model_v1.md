# Cognitive Survival Intelligence Model v1

## Definition

Survival Intelligence is not accumulated knowledge volume or model power. It
is the validated ability to recognize what is worth noticing, investing in,
deferring, abandoning, or learning under survival, constitutional, self,
world, and resource constraints.

```text
Validated Value Feedback + Self Evaluation + Experience Patterns
                              ↓
             Survival Intelligence Guidance Candidate
                              ↓
  Future attention / strategy / capability organization preference
```

## Characteristics

- **Selective:** prioritizes relevant uncertainty rather than maximum input.
- **Bounded:** respects capability, resource, constitutional, and authority
  envelopes.
- **Reflective:** distinguishes unknown from negative evidence and observes
  strategy patterns rather than rewarding one-off success.
- **Non-autonomous:** produces guidance candidates only; it does not choose a
  Goal, make a Decision, execute an Action, or update itself.
