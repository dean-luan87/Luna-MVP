# Cognitive Behavior Candidate Boundary Alignment v1

## Boundary-to-candidate relation

`Behavior Boundary -> Behavior Candidate` means that every Behavior Candidate must cite a boundary reference and remain inside its allowed, restricted, unknown, and evidence-required constraints. It is explicitly not `Behavior Boundary -> Action`.

For a dark room, a Boundary may keep small-scope exploration and information acquisition available while restricting rapid entry. Candidates can then include “request illumination,” “move slowly,” or “wait for assistance.” A future Decision layer may select none, one, or another candidate; this planning layer does not choose.

## Candidate-space rule

Candidate generation expands a managed choice space only. It cannot widen the Behavior Boundary, convert a restricted item into an allowed item, infer Permission from an affordance, or treat an unknown behavior as approved.

## Decision separation

`Candidate Space -> Future Decision Selection -> Future Action Candidate` preserves three independent governance points. This layer is not a Decision Engine, Action Controller, or Permission System.
