# A-Route Execution–Outcome Alignment Validation Model v1

## Stage and scope

Level 3 validates the candidate-only logic across:

```text
Situation → Decision Candidate → Execution Boundary → Outcome Evidence
→ Prediction–Reality Difference → Cause Attribution → Adaptation Candidate
```

It does not execute a real Action, control hardware, call a Provider, enter a
Runtime, perform online learning, adopt Experience, or enter B Reflection.
Fixtures represent deterministic boundary/status/outcome evidence; they do not
simulate a device or claim real execution.

## Frozen principles

| Principle | Required interpretation | Forbidden interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Decision ≠ Execution | a reasonable candidate may encounter execution deviation | outcome alone proves decision wrong |
| Outcome ≠ Failure | outcome is governed reality feedback requiring interpretation | binary success/failure shortcut |
| Past Available Context | decision review uses information available when formed | hindsight invalidates a past decision |
| Cause Attribution | candidate explanation distinguishes factors without blame | one-factor certainty or responsibility assignment |
| Correction Boundary | outcome may form an adaptation candidate | automatic change to Goal, Self Model, or Decision Policy |

## Difference and attribution taxonomy

Difference analysis may retain Information Gap, Understanding Gap, Capability
Gap, Execution Gap, Environment Change, Random Event, and Unknown. Several
causes may coexist. The taxonomy does not establish truth, responsibility, or
an adopted lesson.

Capability Gap remains an explicit candidate classification; it is not a Decision Failure claim.

## Level 3 acceptance focus

The validation checks Decision–Outcome separation, candidate cause
localization, capability awareness, environmental adaptation, and correction
safety. It does not require every represented action to succeed.
