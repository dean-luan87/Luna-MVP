# Cognitive Outcome Evaluation Model v1

## Evaluation candidate

Outcome Evaluation Candidate compares expected and observed outcome candidates under Context, Field, Survival, Mission, uncertainty, alternative explanations, and evidence quality. It can indicate a difference, unexpected event, apparent success/failure candidate, or unresolved result.

Success does not prove the preceding reasoning was correct; failure does not prove the reasoning or future Learning Candidate is negative. Outcome may be contingent, incomplete, or explained by alternative Field conditions.

Evaluation cannot establish truth, rewrite decision history, select behavior, trigger action, mutate State, or promote a pattern to a rule.
