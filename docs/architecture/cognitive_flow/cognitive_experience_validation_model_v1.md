# Experience Validation Model v1

## Validation candidates

Experience validation is a future evidence-quality model, not Learning execution. It plans:

- **Repetition Check**: determine whether similar candidate patterns recur;
- **Context Similarity Check**: preserve whether contexts are materially comparable;
- **Alternative Explanation Check**: retain other possible causes;
- **Confidence Decay**: retain that older or weak evidence may become less relevant; and
- **Exception Detection**: retain candidate deviations rather than forcing a pattern.

No check promotes an Experience Candidate to Fact, Rule, Memory, or automatic adaptation. Validation results remain traceable candidates requiring future governance.
