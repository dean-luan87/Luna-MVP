# Decision Resolution Reversibility Model v1

## Preference candidate

Reversibility Preference Candidate can describe why a bounded, exit-capable, or easily redirected behavior option deserves retention under uncertainty relative to a less reversible alternative. It is a candidate comparison dimension, not a mandate to choose the most reversible option.

In an unknown area, further observation, limited approach, deferral, or seeking assistance may remain alongside deeper entry. The resolution layer retains the reversibility relationship without executing, selecting, or granting Permission.

## Boundary

Reversibility does not prove safety, negate a Mission/Goal, authorize risk, or override Survival. Missing reversibility knowledge remains explicit uncertainty.
