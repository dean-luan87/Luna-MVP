# Outcome Self Capability Feedback Interface v1

```text
Outcome Interpretation Candidate
        ↓
Capability Gap Candidate
        ↓
Self Capability Limitation Candidate
        ↓
Validation
        ↓
Future Self Capability Context Candidate
```

Repeated, validated evidence of low-light visual limitation may form a Self
Capability Limitation Candidate. It does not directly lower a Self Model field
or convert environment risk into a self limitation.
