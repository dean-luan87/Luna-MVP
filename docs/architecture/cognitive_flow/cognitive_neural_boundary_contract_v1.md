# Cognitive Neural Boundary Contract v1

## Contract

The Cognitive Neural Layer is a governed signal boundary between Brain, Middleware, and Capability/Hardware Body. It may transport, classify, validate, trace, and candidate-adapt signals. It may not become another Brain, another Middleware, or an executor.

## Permitted

- transmit candidate-shaped signals on defined Neural Paths;
- classify Attention, Capability, Evidence, State, Reflex, Adaptation, and Simulation signals;
- validate schema/version/compatibility/trace/authority envelope;
- preserve source, destination, lifecycle, provenance, uncertainty, and priority metadata;
- surface compatibility, failure, and evolution-proposal candidates;
- mediate signal adaptation only under approved protocol evolution governance.

## Forbidden

- produce or override a Goal;
- determine or allocate Attention;
- judge a fact or confirm truth;
- execute a model, hardware device, capability, task, or action;
- change Context, Workspace, Simulation, Evaluation, Memory, Experience, or Self State;
- mutate Reducer State;
- self-modify signal authority, safety boundary, or protocol schema at runtime;
- convert Reflex Signal into a World Action.

## Cross-layer contract

| Source | Neural Layer may receive | Neural Layer may emit | Must not emit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brain | cognitive intent/context/attention/simulation candidates | classified capability/transport signal | device command, decision, truth |
| Middleware | capability/resource/reliability/failure candidates | classified response/feedback signal | goal/attention override |
| Provider / Hardware | raw output, health, safety, resource observations | perception/reflex/state signal candidate | world action or cognitive conclusion |
| Cognitive Governance | protocol authority/safety/version constraints | compatibility/evolution proposal candidate | autonomous adoption |

## State and authority invariant

All Neural Layer outputs remain candidates. Reducer remains the only State Mutation Authority. Candidate transport does not upgrade a signal into reality, knowledge, decision, permission, action, or persistent State.
