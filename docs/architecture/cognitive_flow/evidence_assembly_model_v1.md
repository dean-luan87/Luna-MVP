# Evidence Assembly Model v1

## Purpose

Evidence Assembly combines independent Evidence Packages into a bounded Reality
Candidate without turning fragments into certainty.

## Assembly operations

- **Entity Binding:** visual `person` and audio `greeting` may form one Person
  Candidate when time, space, and provenance are compatible.
- **Temporal Alignment:** a vehicle's earlier and current positions may form a
  movement trend candidate.
- **Spatial Association:** an object, location, and distance may form a spatial
  relation candidate.
- **Conflict Preservation:** incompatible evidence remains represented as a
  conflict; it is not silently discarded.

## Output

```text
Evidence Packages
  ↓
Entity / Event / Relation Candidate
  ↓
Reality Candidate
```

A Reality Candidate carries supporting evidence, conflicting evidence, confidence,
unknowns, temporal window, spatial scope, and provenance. Assembly does not write
World State, decide Reality, create a Situation, or produce Action.
