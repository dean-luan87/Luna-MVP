# Cognitive Behavior Candidate Survival Alignment v1

## Survival filter

Every future Behavior Candidate is constrained by Survival before it can remain in the candidate space. The filter can identify a candidate as restricted, evidence-dependent, or lower-risk relative to another candidate; it cannot choose or execute a behavior.

In a high-risk unknown area, entering, waiting, withdrawing, and acquiring information may coexist as candidates with different risk and reversibility descriptions. Survival records the constraint condition, not an Action command.

## Preserved rule

`Survival != Action Controller`. Survival does not resolve a final Decision, grant Permission, make Risk a Fact, or bypass L0/L1 Governance. It ensures the candidate space preserves safety-sensitive alternatives and explicit uncertainty.
