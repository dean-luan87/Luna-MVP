# Information Seeking Behavior Model v1

## Purpose

When Field Understanding Confidence is low, Luna may construct an Information
Source Candidate. The candidate identifies where additional Evidence might be
available; it does not decide or execute.

## Candidate sources

In an unfamiliar mall or station, candidates may include:

- service desk or staffed information point;
- signs, maps, and exit markers;
- verified user input;
- visible entrance/exit structure;
- stable crowd-flow boundary;
- another governed Capability source.

Each source candidate has expected information value, access uncertainty,
resource cost, risk, provenance, and expiry. A source does not become a Fact
until Evidence passes the Evidence Gateway and Reducer.

## Attention and Capability

Information Seeking can produce Attention Priority Candidate and Capability
Requirement Candidate. Field + Task + Unknown determine what to seek; the
Provider cannot choose the objective. Brain may review conflicts or high-risk
choices.

No automatic planning, Prediction, Action, Role, Emotion, Social Relationship,
B Route, or Runtime execution is implemented.

Information Source Candidate and Capability Requirement Candidate remain
candidates. No Prediction, No Action, No Role, No Emotion, No Social
Relationship, and No B Route are implemented.

No Social Relationship is implemented.
